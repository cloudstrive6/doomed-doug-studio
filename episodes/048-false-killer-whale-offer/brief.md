## 7. Risks
- **Title delivery (highest risk).** "Offers Its Kill to Humans" must be proven by sourced cases. Current evidence: Kona 1984 (McSweeney) and the
  1980s British Columbia whale, both reported by Robin Baird (2016 book, Cascadia) and repeated in press coverage. The `ideas.md` note "documented in
  *Marine Mammal Science*" is **not confirmed** for the offering (the MMS papers here are Palacios & Mate 1996 on the sperm whale attack, Baird et al. 2008
  and Zaeschmar et al. 2013). The writer must source the offering to Baird/Cascadia directly or a primary account. If it can't be sourced beyond press
  coverage, stop and tell me: the title changes before the script is approved.
- **Orca confusion:** say once, early, that the false killer whale is a dolphin and not a small orca; "hunts like an orca" is a comparison (group hunting,
  big prey, sharing), not a claim they are related more closely than they are. No "even orcas fear it" style claims.
- **Gore:** cartoon only. No blood in the water, no bitten flesh, no wounded sperm whales drawn (the attack is shown as splashes and fins). The longline tuna
  heads are clean outline drawings. Stranded whales never drawn dying; item 7 is a sober card.
- **Kid appeal:** dolphins and "the whale gave him a present" can drift cute. Keep it adult-coded and deadpan: no cute voices, no hearts, no "aww" framing,
  no baby animals on screen.
- **Conservation honesty:** the Hawaii insular population is Endangered; humans are the bigger threat to them (item 4 honesty line). No claim that false
  killer whales hurt people unless sourced (we have none); Doug's deaths are slapstick, never "it attacked a person".
- **No safety/medical advice:** no "what to do if a whale offers you a fish", no stranding-rescue instructions.
- **Strong sources needed:** Palacios & Mate 1996 (attack details), NOAA species page (size, range, prey, ESA status, current insular abundance), Baird
  et al. 2008, Zaeschmar et al. 2013, Perryman & Foster 1980 (or drop item 2's eating-dolphins half), Mar del Plata 1946 count, Owen 1846 / Kiel 1861,
  Baird 2016 and Cascadia for sharing and the offers.
- **Repetition:** the prey-offering beat must not be staged like 021 (no fish into a boat). One 037 callback maximum.
