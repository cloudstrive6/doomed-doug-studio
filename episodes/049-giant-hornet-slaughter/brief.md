## 7. Risks
- **Title delivery (highest risk).** The "30" and "30,000" and the "afternoon" (hours) must come from an agency or extension source (WSDA, USDA APHIS, WSU or
  Penn State Extension) or from the primary literature (Matsuura & Yamane 1990; Ono et al. 1995). If the best source says "a few dozen" or "a few hours",
  the title changes to match. Tell me before the script is approved. Never use a figure that only appears in viral posts.
- **Insect gore:** cartoon only. No severed bee heads, no bee body parts, no "pile of heads" imagery. Dead bees are whole round cartoon bees with X eyes.
  The meat ball is a round bundle. Stings on people are never drawn as wounds or swelling.
- **Medical/safety advice:** none. No sting first aid, no allergy guidance, no "how to survive a hornet attack", no "what to do if you see one". The
  Washington story can mention that the agency asked the public to report sightings, as history, not as an instruction.
- **Fear-mongering and the nickname:** "murder hornet" is said once, as a past media nickname. The script states that it was eradicated in the US
  (December 2024) and that one sting is rarely the danger (only if sourced. Otherwise just give the sourced death statistics without spin).
  Item 7 is a sober card and is not played for laughs.
- **Kid appeal:** bees, costumes and honey can drift into a nursery tone. Keep it adult-coded and deadpan: no smiling bees, no honey-pot jokes,
  no cute voices, no "aww".
- **Species accuracy:** the slaughter and occupation behaviour is documented for *V. mandarinia* in Japan; the heat ball is *Apis cerana japonica* (Ono 1995;
  the related *Apis cerana* in China shows similar balling. Say "Japanese honeybees"). Use the current name, northern giant hornet, and mention
  the older "Asian giant hornet" once (010 used it).
- **Strong sources needed:** WSDA hornet pages and 2020–2024 press releases (nest counts, queens, the tagging, eradication), USDA APHIS, Ono et al. 1995 *Nature*
  (heat ball, 46 °C), Matsuura & Yamane 1990 (hunting/slaughter/occupation phases), Abe et al. 1991 (larval saliva), the ESA 2022 common-name decision,
  MHLW vital statistics, Xinhua/major outlets for Shaanxi 2013.
- **Repetition:** this is the third distance-like axis recently (043 distance to home, 048 distance to Doug), so this one is **target size**, not distance.
  010 hornet item gets a callback only. Three `animals` episodes in a row are a deliberate one-off (CD decision 11-15).
