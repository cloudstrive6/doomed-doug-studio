# Visual review: 049-giant-hornet-slaughter

## Round 1: pre-render keyframes (2026-11-16)

Scope: `build/contact/sheet_01..17.png` (all 202 shots + endslate), plus full-size spot checks of
s003, s051, s085, s109, s133, s154, s158, s163, s191, s193, s194.

VERDICT: FAIL

The episode is mostly in good shape. Doug is on-model in every shot (red cap, white head; the bee costume,
hornet costume and beekeeper suit all keep the cap and face visible). The section cards and counter are
consistent, and the counter goes up correctly at each death (368 > 369 at s022, 370 at s068, 371 at s111,
372 at s134, 373 at s172, 374 at s193). There is no gore: the meat ball, the X-eyed bodies, the ghosts and
the RIP stones are all cartoon. The Toll section (s136-s151) handles real human deaths with a plain,
sombre treatment, and Doug holding his cap (s150-s151) fits it. Nothing reads as a kids' show. Three
things block the render.

### Required fixes

1. **s158 (illustrator / director): the detection marker is in the wrong place.** The red "!" sits on the
   south coast of Alaska. The narration ("first time... detected in North America") follows s156-s157,
   which pin Nanaimo on Vancouver Island, BC. Move the "!" (or a red dot that matches s156/s157) onto
   Vancouver Island / southwest BC, about where the Strait of Georgia meets the coast. Viewers in BC and
   Washington will catch this. While you are there, also lift "FIRST TIME" so it sits fully below the map
   frame and doesn't straddle the frame's bottom border.
2. **s003 (director): text overlaps the drawing in the cold open.** "200 QUEENS" runs over the split
   tree's left plank, so "EENS" is printed on top of the wood. This is the third shot of the video, which
   is where viewers decide whether to stay. Either move the text left or up so it clears the tree, or
   shift the tree/nest group right by about 150 px. Remove the floating red "?", which has no anchor.
3. **s172 (director / illustrator): Doug's death has no cause on screen.** Doug lies dead by the booth
   (counter goes 372 > 373), but no hornet is visible anywhere in the frame, so the death reads as
   random. Add the hornet from s171, either flying off past the boom gate or perched on the NO HORNETS
   sign, so the gag "Hornets don't carry passports" lands as a sting.
4. **s001 (graphic designer): thumbnail still pending.** The first tile is the "(thumbnail pending)"
   placeholder. The thumbnail has to be in place (it is also the opening image) before the final render.
   This is a pipeline step rather than an art defect.

### Recommended (non-blocking, do if cheap)

5. **s028-s033 (director): variety.** Six near-identical frames in a row: hive in the centre, costumed
   Doug on the left, hornet on the right, sky/grass. It is the same layout from s025, so the run is
   effectively nine shots. Break it up: for example, make s029 (gland) a close-up of the hornet's abdomen
   tip on the hive wall without Doug, and make s031 a wide shot with the tree nest far left and hornets
   streaming along a dashed line.
6. **s147-s150 (director): variety.** Four consecutive "42 DEAD / ABOUT 1,600 INJURED" cards. The tone is
   right, but give s149 ("experts... not sure why that year was worse") its own picture, such as a simple
   bar row of years with 2013 standing tall and a red "?".
7. **s051-s052 (director): hornets clip the hive.** In the second row of the hornet grid, the two right
   hornets are drawn over the hive lid. Raise the grid about 60 px, or deliberately perch them on the lid.
8. **s191 (director): wrong speech-bubble target.** The "for science" bubble's tail points at the tree
   plank, not Doug. Re-aim the tail at Doug's head.
9. **s121 (illustrator): honeybee's lost stinger is unclear.** It is a bare black dash with a red arrow
   and doesn't read. Draw it as a small barbed stinger, or show the bee with a small "torn" mark at its
   tail.
10. **Costume continuity (director, optional):** Doug switches between the bee costume (s004-s011,
    s014-s018) and plain Doug (s012-s013, s019-s020) before s021 explains the costume. If plain Doug is
    the intended "explainer" mode, that's fine. Otherwise keep him in costume from s004 through s024.

### B "IT BRINGS FRIENDS" / "Why One Giant Hornet Can Kill 40 Bees a Minute": PASS

  hornets supports the text, the deadpan Doug in the bee costume is readable at the hive, and the bottom
  wordart is fully legible. It pairs well with "One Giant Hornet" in the title. No gore, no kid-appeal risk.
- Polish (optional): the brief asked for arms folded, but Doug's arms are hidden by the costume. That is fine
  as it stands.

### C "BABY FOOD" / "Why Giant Hornets Can Kill 30,000 Bees to Feed Their Young": FAIL
1. **Thumbnail C, text crop (graphic designer, required).** The black outline of the "D" in "BABY FOOD" runs
   to x = 1279, so it touches the right edge of the frame and is cut off. Shrink the wordart by about 6%, or
   move it about 30 px left, to leave a margin of at least 24 px on the right.
2. **Thumbnail C, larvae read as eggs (illustrator/graphic designer, required).** At 168 px the three
   `hornet_larva_cell` drawings look like white eggs or dumplings in hexagons, not hungry larvae. The
   "open-mouthed larvae" in the brief are missing. Draw a dark open mouth on each brown head, turned up
   toward the hornet. Keep them faceless otherwise, with no eyes and no smiles. Together with "BABY FOOD" on
   hot pink, cute blank blobs risk a nursery look. Open mouths make the image read as hungry and grim.
3. **Thumbnail C, bundle merges with head (graphic designer, recommended).** The brown meat ball overlaps
   the hornet's head and mandibles, so at feed size the two become one brown mass. Move the ball about 40 px
   down and right so it hangs clearly from the mandibles, with pink background showing between it and the
   head. Keep it a plain round bundle with no parts.
- Otherwise OK: no blood or body parts, Doug is on-model and readable at the left, and the text complements
  the title ("Feed Their Young" leads to "BABY FOOD").

Re-check needed: C only, after fixes 1-3.
