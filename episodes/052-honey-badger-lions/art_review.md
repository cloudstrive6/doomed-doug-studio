## Fixes made (art director)
1. **`badger_skin_cutaway`: gore risk, fixed.** The body layer under the skin band was maroon `#9a4a56` with dark-red `#7a3440` speckle,
   under a jagged white-edged window. It read as torn raw meat. I changed it to the art bible's cartoon interior pink `#e79aa0`, removed the
   dark speckle, and moved the skin's inner edge line to `#a8505a` so the thick throat band and the thin belly line still separate. Anchor,
   footprint and the "6 MM" and "BELLY: THIN" arrow points are unchanged.
2. **`receptor_lock_diagram`: the nerve terminal floated, fixed.** The nerve was a 20-unit stub, so the lilac bulb read as a second purple
   blob next to `toxin_key`. I extended the stalk up to local y -430 (screen y ~253 at (800, 640) x 0.9, clear of the caption bar and the HUD).
   The bulb, the gap and the keyhole are unchanged. The footprint top is now -430 instead of -284; the s006-s009, s014 and s015 placements
   need no change.
3. **THE WEIGH-IN label case.** `PUFF ADDER: UP TO ~6 KG` was the only HUD label in capitals (every other one uses `kg`). I changed it to
   `~6 kg` in all 22 shots. The big on-screen callouts (`~126 KG`, `9 KG`...) stay in capitals.
