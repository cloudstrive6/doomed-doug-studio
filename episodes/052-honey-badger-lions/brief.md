  predators fifteen times heavier.

    "Walruses Are Terrifying... Here's Why" **2.58M**, "Why Sperm Whales Are The Most Bizarre Deep-Sea Creatures" **2.01M** (competitors

- **Lane (rule c).** Real predator-prey ecology, venom and the cost of a fight. That is nature horror. The axis is the attacker's weight, not an
  era or deep-time axis.
- **The honest angle is the hook.** The meme says the honey badger is fearless and nothing kills it. The research says something else: leopards
  and lions do kill honey badgers, and badgers mostly avoid fights. What makes predators give up is cost: the skin, the bite and the smell. The
  episode delivers "lions think twice", never "lions can't".

## 7. Risks
- **Gore.** Bites, stings and big-cat kills are the subject.
  - No wounds, no blood, no bite close-ups.
  - The groin-bite claim (if sourced) is said in one flat line and never drawn.
  - The stink is a green cartoon cloud.
  - Doug's deaths are cut to black, X eyes or a flat suit, with the cap on top.
  - Never show a badger being killed. Item 5's fact is stated, and the leopard lands on Doug.
- **Kid appeal and meme traffic (high on this topic).** The honey badger is a 2011 viral meme and a cartoon favourite.
  - Adult-coded deadpan.
  - No "don't care" quotes, no meme voice, no cute badger faces, no nursery tone.
  - No captive or rehab animals: no named individuals, no escape-artist zoo stories.
- **Animal cruelty and policy.** No badger-baiting, dog fights or retaliatory killing methods. Item 6 says beekeepers killed badgers in one
  sourced line, then moves to the fix.
- **Title delivery.** "Think twice" must be delivered literally: item 7 shows a lion giving up, sourced. Item 5 and item 8 keep the honest "they do
  sometimes kill them".
- **Overclaims to avoid:**
  - "immune to venom" (it is resistance; see 045);
  - "fearless" or "the most fearless animal" (Guinness-style listicle claims; cut);
  - the honeyguide partnership stated as fact (it is the debunk);
  - skin thickness numbers without a primary source;
  - "can't be killed by lions";
  - "badgers attack lions for no reason".
- **No safety advice:** nothing about what to do near a badger, snake, bees or a big cat. The hive stand is conservation history, not a how-to.
- **Strong sources needed:**
  - Begg et al. 2003 (*J. Zool.*, diet and foraging) and Begg et al. 2005 (*J. Zool.*, life history and spatial organisation);
  - Drabeck, Dean & Jansa 2015 (*Toxicon*);
  - Dean, Siegfried & MacDonald 1990 (*Conservation Biology*) and Spottiswoode et al. 2016 (*Science*);
  - a citable puff adder collapse record;
  - the Endangered Wildlife Trust / Beggs badger-friendly beekeeping sources;
  - *Mammals of Africa* (or the IUCN assessment) for masses;
  - a primary source for a lion abandoning a badger.
  Never a listicle, a meme page or a viral clip alone for a number or a behaviour.
- **Repetition:** the screener should fail any line that repeats 045's receptor sentence or an 047 wolverine beat.
