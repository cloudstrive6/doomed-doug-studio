## 1. Facts

### FAIL items (new in draft 2)
**F5. The Mosasaur's frame line is false by the episode's own list.** Line 64: "Most stops on this list come after the megalodon's time, but this one is long before it".
- With 11 stops, only 5 are after its time: Supernova, Orcas, Ice Age, Canal and Trench.
- 3 are before it (Mosasaur, Ichthyosaur, Hothouse), and 3 are in its own time (Nursery, Rival, Last Megalodon).
- 5 of 11 is not "most".
- **Fix:** change it to, for example, "Five stops on this list come after the megalodon's time. This one is long before it, in a warm, shallow sea that once covered what is now the Netherlands." That also brings the sentence down from 30 words.

**F6. The Trench twist is now unsourced.** Line 372: "The teeth that started the rumour are some of the best evidence against it."
- Draft 1 leaned on the two cut lines to support this. Draft 2 is left with only "crusts grow slowly", which shows the 1959 date was wrong. It does not show the teeth are "some of the best evidence" against survival.
- facts.md has no row for this claim, and Southern Fried Science doesn't say it. The NHM page says only that the 1959 method was disproven in the 1970s and that the teeth fall "well within megalodon's expected timeline".
- **Fix:** reword it to what NHM supports. For example: "But that's not the twist. Re-checked, the teeth that started the rumour turned out to be as old as every other megalodon tooth."
- Add a facts.md row citing https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/is-megalodon-still-alive.html ("disproven in the 1970s"; "well within megalodon's expected timeline").
- Cite the same NHM page for line 368, "it didn't hold up".
