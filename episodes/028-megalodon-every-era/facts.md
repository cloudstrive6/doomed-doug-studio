## Brief leads that changed
1. **Birth size.** Shimada et al. 2021 (*Historical Biology*) estimated about 2 m. Shimada et al. 2025 (*Palaeontologia
   Electronica* 28(1):a12) re-analysed vertebral growth bands and suggests 3.6-3.9 m. The cold open uses the newer figure with
   "may have been ... almost four meters"; item 1 gives both ("about two meters ... closer to four"). Draft 2: the same 2025
   abstract says that birth size makes "less likely the need for nursery areas", so item 1's twist now says the newest study
   thinks pups that big may not have needed a nursery, and the kicker is hedged ("probably home").
2. **Womb line.** The 2025 abstract names the behaviour as "intrauterine oophagous behavior" (embryos eating unhatched eggs);
   2021 press coverage says babies "likely grew by eating eggs in womb". The script says "after feeding on unhatched eggs in
   the womb", hedged by "may have", not "ate their siblings".
3. **Supernova distance and timing.** Wallner 2016 gives sources "up to 100 parsecs" and influxes at 1.5-3.2 and 6.5-8.7 Myr.
   The 150 light-year figure is the 2017 KU revision. The script says "one estimate" and "between about one and a half and
   three million years ago".
4. **Ice Age twist.** "Too cold to follow" was dropped: secondary sources give megalodon an inferred tolerance that included
   cool water (and Griffiths 2023 says endothermy helped it into cooler seas). The twist is habitat loss at low sea level.
5. **Hothouse.** No study put megalodon in PETM water. Two sourced numbers are compared (above 36 °C water; about 27 °C
   body), arithmetic 36 - 27 = 9, and the verdict is labelled our reasoning.
6. **Challenger depth.** The 5 km figure is Station 252, 2,740 fathoms (5,011 m), a *Carcharodon* tooth inside a manganese
   nodule. The script says "one came from about five kilometers down" about shark teeth in general, and gives no depth for
   the two megalodon teeth dated in 1959.
7. **Cut:** Ichthyotitan diet (no source); *Cretoxyrhina* line (not opened; budget).
8. **Writer option used:** The Rival (Livyatan) added, because the 10-item draft was under 2,900 words. Age is the re-dated
   9.9-8.9 Myr, not the older 12-13 Myr.
