## Required fixes
1. **Rule 9: bring the average sentence length to at least 14.0** by the writer's own counting. At about 2,077 words that means 148 sentences or fewer, about 12 merges. Do not merge punch lines (the bible forbids fixing it that way). Keep every sentence at 35 words or fewer, and keep item 1's name at word 34 or earlier. Merges of non-punch connective sentences that are available:
   - Line 150: "Chimps appear on the ridge. Ten. Twenty. Twenty-seven." becomes "Chimps appear on the ridge: ten, twenty, twenty-seven." (saves 3, and the count still reads aloud)
   - Line 106: "...about a hundred and ten. It may be the first..." becomes "...about a hundred and ten, and it may be the first..." (saves 1)
   - Line 98: "The colobus do fight back. When chimps attack, ..." becomes "The colobus do fight back, because when chimps attack, ..." (saves 1)
   - Line 346: "Ngogo had about two hundred members. In twenty fifteen, ..." becomes "Ngogo had about two hundred members, and in twenty fifteen ..." (saves 1)
   - Line 354: "...and seventeen of the smallest. Some of those chimps..." becomes "..., and some of those chimps had been on friendly terms for years." (saves 1, 33 words)
   - Line 322: "...extremely rare. Nobody had ever recorded an exile this long, either." becomes "..., and nobody had ever recorded an exile this long." (saves 1)
   - Line 248: "...far away. But if they find a stranger..." becomes "..., but if they find a stranger and outnumber it, they attack." (saves 1)
   - Line 378 (outro): "Nobody will cross for it. It belongs to both sides." becomes "Nobody will cross for it, because it belongs to both sides." (saves 1; keep the outro at 30 words or fewer)
   - Fix 2 below saves 2 more.
   - Re-run the count and update the line 380 footer.
2. **Rule 4 kickers of 8-25 words:**
   - Line 158: "...and the next relative split later. It wears a red cap." Make the kicker one sentence of 8 words or more. For example: "The gorilla line split from the chimp line about eight to ten million years ago, and the next relative to branch off wears a red cap." That is 27 words, so trim the first clause to stay at 25 or fewer. Or: "The next relative split off later, and it wears a red cap."
   - Line 374: "The family tree doesn't protect anyone. It just tells you who's next." Make it one sentence, for example "The family tree doesn't protect anyone, it just tells you who's next." or "...anyone; it just tells you who's next."
3. **Line 142: logic contradiction.** "The disturbing part is which gorilla they went for. Size didn't decide it. Numbers did. Both times, the gorilla that died was one of the smallest in its group." "It" refers to which gorilla they went for, so the line says size didn't decide the target and then that the smallest one died. (The draft 1 review's suggested wording caused this.) Change "Size didn't decide it." to "Size didn't decide the fight." Keep "Numbers did."
