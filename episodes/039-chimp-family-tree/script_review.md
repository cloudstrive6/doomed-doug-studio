## Required fixes
1. **Item 1, line 37**: "...and often chew the tip into a point." The cited source says "sometimes gnawing on the ends to sharpen them". Change "often" to "sometimes", and update the facts.md correction note, which wrongly lists "often" as the fix.
2. **Item 5, line 213**: "...so a seventy-storey building could stand on the bottom and stay underwater." The 3 m per storey in facts.md is a low residential figure. Real 70-storey towers are about 245-300 m, deeper than the 220 m river, so the comparison is false. Swap in an analogy that fits under 220 m with margin and show the arithmetic in facts.md. Either "two football pitches stood on end would still be underwater" (2 × 105 m = 210 m) or "a fifty-storey building" (about 4 m per storey = 200 m).
3. **Item 2, line 105**: "...it may be the first recorded case of one primate driving down another's numbers." Humans are primates and have driven down many primate populations. The paper says *nonhuman* primate. Change to: "...the first recorded case of an animal other than us driving down another primate's numbers." Keep "may be".
4. **Opening, line 25**: "By the end, Doug is a chimpanzee standing on the line where one chimp family split in two." Add the lethal stake so the open loop pays. Either "...split in two and started killing each other." or the brief's "...and both halves have decided he's on the other side." Keep it under about 25 words so it still lands by 0:10.
5. **Item 3, line 141**: "But size didn't decide it. Numbers did." Add a flagged twist marker (rule 4) that isn't next to a "Here's the..." or "The worst part is" marker. For example: "The disturbing part is who they went for. Size didn't decide it. Numbers did."
6. **Doug-beat length and boss length** (rule 4: Doug beat 15-30 words; retention rule 1: items 200-260 words):
   - Item 3, line 149: replace "while the narrator counts the chimps on the ridge up to twenty-seven" with an actual short count. For example: "Chimps appear on the ridge. Ten. Twenty. Twenty-seven." Bring the beat to 30 words or fewer.
   - Item 2, line 109: cut "The chimps look at the males, then at Doug, and decide to try a different tree" to something like "The chimps pick a different tree."
   - Item 9, line 367: trim to 30 words or fewer, for example by dropping "Doug, in the chimp costume,". Then trim the boss to 260 words or fewer. The Gombe pair (lines 353-355) can merge into one sentence.
