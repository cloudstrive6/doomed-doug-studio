# 041 Grizzly Won't Fight: asset requests (director, 2026-11-09)

33 new drawings. House style as usual: creatures and props in the detailed tier (thick outline 4-6, flat fills, interior shading),
humans in the crude stick tier (`auto_ink`). Nothing cute, so no big shiny eyes and no smiles on animals (brief: kid-appeal risk).
No blood and no wounds anywhere. Sizes are local units at scale 1. "Anchor bottom-centre" means feet or wheels sit on y=0.
The shotlist already uses these names, positions and scales, so keep the anchors and rough sizes below. If one has to change,
tell the director.
