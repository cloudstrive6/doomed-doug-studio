## Round 1: keyframes (pre-render), 2026-11-02

I checked all 19 contact sheets (s001 to s217 plus the endslate) and opened full-size keyframes for s009, s013, s106, s142 and s208. s001 shows "thumbnail pending" and I did not review it, as instructed.

**What is good.** Doug is on-model (red cap, white head) and readable in every shot where he appears. The counters (DOUG DEATHS 274 to 280, WALRUS COUNT 1, 2, about 300, about 35,000) are correct and advance on the right beats. Every death is a cartoon death: the X-eyes ghost on black, the cap left behind, the dust cloud. Nothing is gory, and the Icy Cape dead-walrus beat (s201 to s202) is text-only, which is the right call. The tone is adult and dry, not like a kids' show. The drawings are recognisable: walrus side, front and swim views, the calf-on-back, the polar bear, the seal, the cow, the boats, the plane and the Empire State Building. Chapter tags, the hook (s002 to s004) and the payoff (s207 to s210) all match the narration.

VERDICT: FAIL

Fixes 1 to 3 block the render. Fixes 4 to 8 are small and should go in the same pass.

1. **s009** (director, illustrator): the clipboard is cluttered and contradicts itself. There is a third red tick under "FOUND DEAD" next to the red X, so scavenging is both ticked and crossed. Both labels also sit on top of the scribble lines. Remove the stray lower tick. Move the "HUNTED" and "FOUND DEAD" labels to the right of the clipboard, outside the paper, with the tick and X kept on the paper next to their rows.
2. **s106** (director): the "BREEDING SEASON" label covers both bulls' heads and eyes. Move it up into the clear sky band (y around 200) or to the top-left, and keep the two faces clear.
3. **s013** (director): "BLUBBER" sits across the walrus's eye, face and whiskers. Move it above the back, into the sky (y around 200, x around 700 to 1100), and point at the stained shoulder patch with a short red arrow if needed.
4. **s052** (director): the red arrow on the right runs through the "TEETH" word. Move "TEETH" right or down so neither arrow crosses it.
5. **s169** (director): the dashed sight lines from the plane run through the "AERIAL SURVEY" label. Move the label above or beside the plane so the dashes start below it.
6. **s207, s208, s209** (illustrator, director): on the "QUIET PLEASE" sign, "PLEASE" touches the bottom edge of the board, and the post runs across Doug's cap and face. Make the board about 20% taller, or shrink the text so it has a margin. Move the post to Doug's hand side so his face stays clear. Keep s210 ("nope") matching.
7. **s127** (director): the "BODY BELOW" label overlaps the walrus's hanging body. Move it to the left of the body, with the arrow pointing in. **s154** (director): "FEMALES AND YOUNG" covers the fifth walrus in the water. Move it below that row or to the right.
