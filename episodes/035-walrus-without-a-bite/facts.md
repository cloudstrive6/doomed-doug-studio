Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Checked 2026-11-02.

How each source was checked:
- **Fetched** means I read the passage on the page with WebFetch.
- **Search** means I confirmed the claim only from search-result text quoting the page (the page itself was blocked, paywalled, or a PDF I could not extract).
- **Secondary** means a news outlet reporting an agency statement or a study.

The screener should re-open the **Search** and **Secondary** rows and the FLAG rows first.
