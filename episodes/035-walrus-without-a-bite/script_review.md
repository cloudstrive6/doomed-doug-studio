## New claims: fact check
- **Normal air pressure about 101 kPa: confirmed.** NOAA JetStream (fetched) gives "1013.25 in both millibars (mb) and hectopascal (hPa)", which is 101.3 kPa.
- **Walrus at -87.9 kPa in air, lower underwater: re-confirmed.** The Kastelein, Muller & Terlouw 1994 abstract gives -87.9 kPa in air and -118.8 kPa underwater on a mackerel.
- **"Almost nine tenths of the air" in a sealed jar: correct.**
  - 87.9 / 101.3 = 0.868 of ambient pressure removed.
  - In a rigid jar at constant temperature, pressure is proportional to the amount of air, so about 87% of the air is gone.
  - "Almost nine tenths" is fair, and the jar is clearly framed as hypothetical ("if that mouth were clamped").
- **Point Lay had 330 people at the 2020 census: confirmed.** Citypopulation.de, from US Census data (fetched), gives 189 in 2010 and 330 in 2020.
- **"About a hundred walruses per person": correct.** 35,000 / 330 = 106. The line names the census year, so pairing the 2014 herd with the 2020 population is stated openly and is not misleading.
