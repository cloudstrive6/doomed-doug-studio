## 2026-11-02, creative director: package gate (thumbnails A, B, C)
No thumbnail round in visual_review.md yet (keyframe rounds only; s001 marked "thumbnail pending"), so this verdict is mine on the PNGs at 1280x720 and *_small. Title: "How the Walrus Kills Doug Without a Single Bite".

All three pass the hard lines: no bite shown or implied, no gore, no harmlessness claim, no WALRUS/BITE/KILLS text, the only count is 35,000, Doug on-model. None is cute enough to be a kids signal, but A and C each carry one fixable risk. All three are clicks for me; each needs a short fix pass.

**Thumbnail A ("WRONG BOAT", opening image): CHANGES (minor).** Best variant: one huge front-facing bull, tusks over the gunwale, method shown, title complemented and not repeated.
1. "WRONG" is cropped: its outline runs to x=1279. Shift the text block left about 60 px (or scale to 0.9) so both words keep a margin of 50 px or more on the right and top. The walrus head ends near x=610, so there is room.
2. Make the boat visibly *tipping*. Rotate the dinghy about 12-15 degrees down toward the walrus (stern under the tusks dipping into the water line, bow up) and add a small water slosh at the low gunwale. Right now it reads as a boat parked next to a walrus.
3. Put the oar in Doug's hands, raised like a useless weapon (brief: "holding an oar"). Keep his scream face.
4. Keep the angry brows (they stop it reading as a cuddly seal), but add 2-3 more pale scar lines on the forehead and cheeks so it reads "old scarred bull" and not "cartoon egg". No smile, no moustache gag (already true; keep it).

**Thumbnail B ("35,000"): CHANGES.** The number plus Doug pinned between the herd and the water is a strong, honest click, and it pays off item 8-9.
1. The herd reads as a parade: identical clones in straight, evenly spaced rows. Break the grid. Vary the scale (back rows smaller, 0.6-0.8), x-offsets and facing a little, let bodies overlap, and fill the back to the horizon with a dense band of humps and heads so it reads as *thousands*, not 40.
2. Add threat and motion: the front 3-4 walruses lean toward Doug with grey dust/gravel puffs and short motion lines behind them. It should read "about to stampede", not "standing in line".
3. The fore flippers read as boots/elephant legs. Use the side-view walrus with splayed fore flippers turned out (library walrus side view used in the keyframes), or hide most of them behind overlap.
4. Keep "35,000" exactly as is (no "40,000", no "+", no "WALRUSES").

**Thumbnail C (4-panel grid): CHANGES.** The grid is honest (TUSKS / BOAT / FIGHT / STAMPEDE are real item names, all non-bite deaths) and readable small.
1. FIGHT: at feed size the two symmetrical bulls nose-to-nose with crossed tusks read as a nuzzle or heart shape, which is a cute/kids risk. Re-stage it: bulls reared up and angled, tusks raised mid-swing (not crossed), and tiny red-capped Doug standing between them, looking up. Every other panel has Doug; this one must too.
2. STAMPEDE: same fix as B, items 1-2 (break the rows, add dust and motion toward Doug).
3. FIGHT background: swap the lilac for a darker grey-blue or dusk orange so the grid is less pastel overall.

Order of preference for the A/B test: A (hero, opening image), then B. C is the third test slot only after its fixes. Graphic designer applies; visual screener runs the thumbnail round on the fixed versions before `packaged`.
