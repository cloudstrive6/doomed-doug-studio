## 1. Facts (I checked 30+ claims myself on the web, plus every surprising number)

**Confirmed against the source:**
- **Kavachi:** summit about 65 ft, roughly 20 m (Smithsonian Magazine). Activity "nearly continuous" since at least 1939. Landsat 9 imaged an eruption in May 2022. Hammerheads and silky sharks were seen in 2015. The Phillips "except maybe bacteria" quote and the "ten degrees higher" and pH drop details are in National Geographic.
- **Crop circles:** the build takes 7-9 days, the structure plays a role in female mate choice, and males never reuse a nest (Kawase et al. 2013). The fish is up to 12 cm long (FishBase max TL) and was found at 10-27 m.
- **Taam Ja':** about 275 m in 2021, then at least 420 m in December 2023 without reaching the bottom. A CTD on a cable was used. There is a Caribbean-like layer near 400 m. The Dragon Hole was 301 m (phys.org).
- **Lost City:** found 4 December 2000. It is nearly 100% carbonate, "the same material as limestone". It is driven by olivine turning into serpentine, not by magma. The origin-of-life idea comes from UW news. Chimneys reach up to 60 m. The field has been active for more than 120,000 years (Ludwig et al. 2011).
- **Bloop:** recorded in summer 1997 and heard over a range of more than 5,000 km. It is consistent with icequakes, probably from between Bransfield Strait and the Ross Sea (NOAA PMEL). It was several times louder than a blue whale and rose quickly in frequency (Wikipedia, quoting NOAA).
- **Upsweep:** present since August 1991, peaks in spring and fall, located near inferred volcanic seismicity. Its origin is "unresolved" (NOAA PMEL).
- **Jacuzzi of Despair:** about 3,300 ft down, about 100 ft around, about 12 ft deep, and 4-5 times saltier than seawater. The team was led by Cordes (Temple University) and the pool kills crabs (DSBSoc / Discovery News).
- **Champagne vent:** found March-April 2004 at about 1,604 m on NW Eifuku. The droplets are more than 98% CO2 and stuck to the ROV "like clumps of grapes". The fluid is 103 °C with pH 3.4-4.8 (NOAA PMEL, Lupton et al.).
- **Okinawa CO2 lake:** 1,380 m at Yonaguni Knoll IV, capped by hydrate, with microbes in the sediment above (Inagaki et al. 2006, *PNAS*).
- **Scaly-foot snail:** listed as Endangered on 25 July 2019 because of mining. It is known from three vent fields, and even exploratory mining could damage vents or smother the snails (Australian Museum).
- **Chapopote:** discovered 2003 at 2,914-3,300 m, with asphalt covering more than 1 km² and *Escarpia* tubeworms in the fissures (MacDonald et al. 2004; Frontiers 2025).
- **Golden orb:** NOAA announced on 22 April 2026 that it is the remnant of dead cells at the base of *Relicanthus daphneae*, at 3,250 m.
- **Dark oxygen:** about 13,000 ft down, potato-sized nodules, up to 0.95 V on a single nodule (Northwestern). The *Nature Geoscience* editor's note is dated 8 April 2026.
- **Hadal communities:** Fendouzhe dived 8 July to 17 August 2024. The communities run along 2,500 km, from 5,800 m to 9,533 m, and the methane is made by microbes (Du et al. 2025, *Nature*, published 30 July 2025).
- **Arithmetic checked:** 160 atm at 1,600 m, 950 atm at 9,533 m, Everest plus 684 m, about 140 soccer fields in 1 km², New York to Los Angeles about 3,940 km, and London to Moscow about 2,500 km.

**FAIL items:**
- **F1, the Sharkcano residency claim is contradicted by the sources.** Line 37 says "They weren't passing through, either; they were living in the mouth of an active volcano." National Geographic describes sharks "darting in and out between the clouds" between eruptions. Phillips calls residency "a lingering question mark". The sources say the sharks' range is unknown and that tagging has not been possible. facts.md gives no source for "weren't passing through". Line 41 repeats the claim: "the surface where the sharks live". The script also contradicts itself, because line 39 says nobody knows "where they go when it erupts". The brief's own twist wording ("They live inside the crater") carried this error in.
- **F2, the Poseidon age in the kicker is unsupported.** Line 151 says "Poseidon stood for a hundred and twenty thousand years". The 120,000 years is the age range of the *field's* carbonate deposits (Ludwig et al. 2011: dated deposits range from "modern to 120,000 years"). Nothing dates Poseidon itself to 120,000 years.
- **F3, a citation fix in facts.md (does not change the script).** The "at least 120,000 years" claim cites Kelley et al. 2005, *Science*. That paper reported about 30,000 years. The 120,000-year figure comes from Ludwig et al. 2011, *Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta*. Correct the citation and URL.

**Advisory (no fix required):** "about twelve centimeters" is the species' maximum length (Kawase 2013 says about 10 cm), so "up to about twelve" would be more exact. UW gives Poseidon as 180 ft and "18 stories". "Sixty meters / twenty-story" is supported by the 60 m figure but sits slightly above UW's number.

- The zone shift comes at about 66%, as the brief decided (the bible's default is 50%). All the brief's gags are present and none extra. Note that the series bible says to use 2-3 running gags, and this script uses about 6. That was the creative director's call in the brief, not a screener fail.
- Small note for the director: the gauge reads "~1,000 M" at both the Bloop and the Jacuzzi, so it does not drop between items 5 and 6. Set the Bloop to about 900 m, or accept the repeat.

## 4. Voice and humour (8/10)
- The narrator is deadpan and consistent, with good dry kickers ("He was standing on it.", "a new shape in the tar is not news", "found someone who wants to keep it"). The humour is adult-coded (the dating joke, the toast) and the narrator stays slightly sadistic but fond.
- Weak spots: "The volcano is not." followed by "The sharks went straight back to circling" doubles up the Sharkcano joke. "Doug has died. Again." followed by a 16-word explainer dilutes the stock gag.

## 5. TTS readiness
- **T1:** "Torquigener albomaculosus" (line 65) is a Latin binomial at the end of a 34-word sentence, which counts as a tongue-twister for Chirp. It adds nothing for viewers, and the script already drops the golden orb's species name.
- **T2 (editor):** "Taam Ja'" has an apostrophe. Respell it in the TTS input ("Taam Ha"), as the pronunciation note says.
- No abbreviations, symbols, parentheses or digits appear in the spoken text.

- **Made-for-kids risk:** PASS. Speech bubbles are 1-2 words and adult-coded, with no nursery framing.
- **Inauthentic content:** PASS. Each item has its own story and the narration is original.
