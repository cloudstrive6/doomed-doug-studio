## Required fixes

1. **Isopod study year (fact).** Quote: "In twenty twenty-five, scientists studying two related giant isopods found a huge stomach and an extremely slow metabolism, which helps explain the waiting."
   - In the script: change "twenty twenty-five" to "twenty twenty-six" (or "Earlier this year"). Keep the word count about the same.
   - In facts.md, row "2025: a study of two related giant isopods": cite *Cell*, 5 June 2026, "Deep-Sea Megafauna Co-Opts Microbial Energy Metabolism Genes to Withstand Ultra-Long Starvation". Add the CAS release https://lssf.cas.cn/en/news/rh/202606/t20260625_5113316.html and the paper DOI, then remove the "Screener: please confirm" note.
2. **False 001 callback (fact).** Quote: "Doug met a snailfish once before, on his first trip to the bottom of the ocean, and this one lives deeper than that one ever went."
   - Delete "and this one lives deeper than that one ever went". It is the same 8,336 m fish from 001.
   - If the callback stays, it must be true, for example that Doug has met this exact fish before.
3. **Item 10 repeats 001's Hadal Snailfish (repetition).** "The Fish Line" re-tells 001 lines 243-245: the protective molecule, the 8,200-8,400 m wall, and the 8,336 m Izu-Ogasawara juvenile.
   - Get a creative-director ruling first, since the brief scoped this item. Either:
     - keep the item and cut the re-explained 001 material to one callback sentence, so the mechanism and twist rest on material 001 didn't use (for example the 2017 to 2022 record creep, the "saltier than the sea" reversal, and the PRESSURE? crossing-out); or
     - replace it with another sourced giant near 8,000 m.
   - Either way, replace "if you set Mount Everest down on the seafloor right here, nearly all of it would be underwater" with a new scale analogy. Everest-in-the-trench is already used in 001 and 006.
4. **TTS: "xenophyophore".** Quote: "It's a xenophyophore, and it's one single cell." Either:
   - add a pronunciation to the header comment (ZEE-no-FY-oh-for), replacing "xenophyophore is never spoken"; or
   - cut the word ("It's a single cell called a xenophyophore" still needs the respelling). Add "foram" to the list as well.
5. **Fish-line precision (fact wording).** Quote: "Their estimate for that point was around eight thousand two hundred to eight thousand four hundred meters." plus "It still hasn't crossed that line." The paper's own estimate is 8,200 m, so 8,336 m has arguably already passed it.
   - Change "It still hasn't crossed that line." to wording that is true at both ends, for example "It's sitting right on that line."
   - Or say the newer estimate is about 8,200 m and that the record is just past it, then adjust the kicker logic.
