## 1. Facts

**New claims (both required this pass):**
- **Mimic size: confirmed.** The line is "about six centimeters long, roughly the length of your thumb … around half a meter".
  - The species description (Norman & Hochberg 2005, *Molluscan Research* 25(2):57-70) gives a mantle length up to 58 mm and a total length over 480 mm. SeaLifeBase and OctoNation repeat these figures, and Wikipedia and EOL give about 60 cm.
  - So "about 6 cm" body and "around half a meter" both hold.
  - Caveat: both are maximum sizes, not typical ones. That is acceptable at "about" and "around", and the two figures are consistent with each other. Optional: "grows to about six centimeters".
  - facts.md should add SeaLifeBase (https://www.sealifebase.ca/summary/100159) as the institutional source next to the OctoNation link (advisory).
