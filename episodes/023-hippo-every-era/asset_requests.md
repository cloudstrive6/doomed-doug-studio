# 023-hippo-every-era: asset requests (director)

All drawings follow the house style (detailed tier for creatures/places, thick dark outlines, flat fills with shading, facing right unless noted). Shots listed are where the shotlist uses each name. Purussaurus, Titanoboa and Allosaurus are also Shorts end-card teases (black silhouette + red outline), so keep their silhouettes recognisable.

## `allosaurus`
Allosaurus fragilis, Late Jurassic theropod, detailed tier. Bipedal, horizontal body and long stiff tail, big narrow head with a small rough crest/horn in front of each eye, many blade teeth along a closed or slightly open jaw, short three-fingered arms with big claws, tan-olive with darker back stripes, pale belly. Not cute. Facing right, bottom-centre anchor (feet y=0), ~760x380. Must read clearly different from `t_rex` (slimmer head, eye horns, longer arms).
- Shots: s165, s166, s167, s177, s178, s181

## `ambulocetus`
Ambulocetus natans, ~3 m 'walking swimming whale' (Eocene). Low crocodile-like body on four short legs with BIG webbed hind feet, long narrow toothy snout (crocodile jaws), small eyes high on the head, long thick tail without flukes, brown-grey with a paler belly. Facing right, bottom-centre anchor (feet y=0), ~600x200. Mid-tone fill so it reads half-submerged on #5cc6c9 shallows. Also used rotated -90 standing on its tail beside a basketball hoop.
- Shots: s088, s094, s095, s096, s097, s099, s102, s105

- Shots: s205, s208
