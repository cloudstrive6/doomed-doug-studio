# Facts: 023 Would the Hippo Survive Every Prehistoric Era? Doug Didn't (working)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Fiction and framing are not claims: Doug, his deaths and the
counter, the time machine and the hippo fitting inside it, the HIPPO SURVIVED tally, the tourist map, the badge, the family photo,
the picnic, the snorkels, the flag, speech bubbles, and the narrator's asides ("counts as yesterday", "the lions had a pulse",
"it has opinions", "rarely been anyone's first choice", "the narrator checked the whole beach"). **Every hippo verdict is the
narrator's judgement from the sourced numbers below, never a study's finding**; the script says so once in item 1 ("nobody has
ever run this experiment, so these are educated guesses from the fossils"). Everyday comparisons are marked "comparison", with the
source for the everyday object and the arithmetic.

Checked 2026-10-19 (draft 1); revised for draft 2 the same day after the screener's review (F1-F5, A1-A4). "Verified" = I read the claim on the page (WebFetch). "Search snippet" = confirmed from search-result
text quoting the page, because the page blocked WebFetch (403) or was not opened. The screener should re-open those.

**Gore check:** no injury words. Bite forces are numbers only. Every Doug death cuts away (roar, splash, shadow, cut to black or white,
ghost float). No hippo is shown dying: losses are a red X stamp or the time-machine door shutting with ears visible.
**Kid-appeal check:** no "dino", no toys or games, no "who would win" wording; the hippo is never cute.
**Myths avoided:** no "deadliest animal in Africa"; no T. rex vision myth; no "Megalania"; Titanoboa diet hedged ("may have eaten
mostly fish", diet disputed); no "hippos are related to pigs"; no Allosaurus pack hunting (the "lots of friends" line was cut).
