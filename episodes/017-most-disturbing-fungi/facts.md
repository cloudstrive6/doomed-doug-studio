## Overstated claims avoided (brief section 7)
- Massospora cathinone: "the researchers think it may help keep the insects going" (hypothesis, as the authors frame it). Psilocybin is not used.
- Gibellula behaviour change: "suspect ... nobody has proven that yet".
- Chytrid 501 / 90: attributed to "a major study in twenty nineteen", and the Lambert et al. 2020 challenge plus the authors' reply are both stated.
- Salem: proposed (Caporael 1976) and rejected (Spanos and Gottlieb 1976); "many historians remain unconvinced".
- Tomb: A. flavus "has been blamed"; the "curse" was a newspaper story.
- Asperigimycins: "only in the lab, on cells, with animal testing next ... a long way from a cure".
- C. auris and climate: "only a hypothesis ... the researchers themselves say it will be hard to prove".
- Gore / medical: no symptoms lists, no treatment, no "see a doctor". Chytrid: "the heart simply stopped" is the only body detail (Voyles abstract: cardiac arrest). Ergot: "the burning it caused" only; no gangrene, no limbs. Valley fever: "lungs" is the only body word.

## Cut or changed during research
- Brief: "5.7-6.7 million by 2012": kept as "at least five point seven to six point seven million" (FWS 2012 release; the headline says "exceeds 5.5 million", body gives the range).
- Brief: "first documented in a cave near Albany": the FWS wording is "some 40 miles west of Albany"; the cave's identity varies between sources (Howes Cave / Haile's Cave), so the script names no cave.
- Brief: Gibellula found "in a cave": it was found in an old Victorian gunpowder store at Castle Espie; later finds are in caves. Script follows the sources.
- Brief: flies prefer dead infected females "more than living ones": not confirmed in the ISME abstract. Cut. Script says the chemicals "draw in healthy males".
- Brief: Gibellula "can't infect people": no source states it. Replaced with the sourced genus fact (Gibellula is exclusively spider-pathogenic).
- Brief: tomb deaths "within a few years": Penn says "within weeks"; script says "within a short time".
- Massospora "keeps calling": no source; cut.
- Massospora relative with psilocybin: cut (optional, drug-risk).
- Draft 2: ergot "not suggested to be a fungus until 1711" cut (screener F1: unverifiable, sources disagree). Massospora resting-spore sentence cut for pacing (first death earlier).
