**FAIL item:**

**F5. The "bison on the road" claims have no source row in facts.md.**
- Three lines claim this as fact:
  - The Record kicker: "in summer the herd likes to graze its way right along the road"
  - The Jam: "In Yellowstone, a traffic jam often has a bison at the front of it, walking down the middle of the road at whatever speed it likes."
  - The Jam: "Herds walk along and across the park roads"
- facts.md cites KTVU for these, but KTVU describes one herd *crossing* the road. It does not say herds walk *along* roads, and it does not say jams are *often* bison-led.
- The claims are true and easy to source. Cowboy State Daily (2025-06-06) says: "Bison jams are a frequent occurrence on the narrow roads running throughout Yellowstone", and describes a bull "straddling both lanes" as a "rustic roadblock". https://cowboystatedaily.com/2025/06/06/its-not-easy-to-get-bison-off-the-road-when-they-dont-want-to/
- **Fix (facts.md only):** add a row covering all three lines with that source, or an NPS or other reliable source that says the same. If no source is added, cut "often" and "along" from the script.
