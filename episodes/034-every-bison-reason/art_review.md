## Fixes (illustrator)
1. **`american_bison_charging`: the gallop has no front legs.** The low head and beard cover the front legs completely. In colour
   you see only the two hind legs plus a stub under the chin. In silhouette (s146, s199-s200 are likely to be read that way) it is a
   two-legged body with a blob at the front. It reads as "sniffing the ground", not "charging". Fix: bring both front legs out
   **in front of and below the head**, one reaching forward (hoof at about x +250..+330, y 0..-40) and one folded under the chest
   (about x +120..+180). Keep the hind legs pushing back and the tail up. Keep the head low with the horn tip at about (+300,-200) and nothing on it. The
   lowest hoof stays on y=0. If the reaching hoof pushes the extents past +340, tell the director. Check it as a silhouette at 0.32
   and rotated -12 (s101) before resubmitting.
2. **`american_bison_face`: remove the smile.** The curved line under the nostrils turns up at both ends, so the close-up reads as
   a faint grin (cute and friendly), against the brief's "flat, calm, faintly unimpressed". Make it a short flat line, or one that
   dips slightly at the ends. Keep the half-lidded eyes.
3. **`american_bison_face`: the flat bottom edge shows in frame.** The body is cut off flat at y +240. At the shotlist placements
   (s049: y 620 at 1.6, so the edge lands at y 1004; s039 at 976; s053 at 998), a straight cut-off line sits on screen and the head
   looks like a bust on a plinth (the same problem as the 027 cut-edge rule). Extend the hump and chest down to **y +340** (with the
   shading continued and the beard unchanged) so it runs off the bottom of frame at every listed placement. Everything above y +240
   stays where it is, so the shotlist needs no re-placement.

The Yellowstone calendar ramp works: one month per item, light winter through summer to the dusk rut. It reuses the bible's
snow-slope (JAN), savanna green, dusk (`#f2a24a` over `#e88a4a`, ground `#b8913a`), death-beat (`#0b0f14`/`#141a14`) and
cream diagram colours exactly, and every river stays surface `#3a9ad9`. Bison read on every sky and ground I tested. The honesty card
`#4a4f5e` is kept: the bull, the grizzly and white text all read on it (tested; a lighter `#6a6f7e` was not needed).
- **A. MAY grass is green**: the wordart goes in the sky with the white/yellow override, as proposed. No wordart on the grass band.
  The sober grey card (`#8a8f94` / `#7a7f84`) currently carries only the black calf silhouette (s085-s086), and that is fine. If any text
  is added there, use black text or white with a 4 px black outline (plain white on `#8a8f94` is only 3.3:1).
- **B. End slate `#2a2030` (s207)**: the seven `bison_head_icon`s at 0.6 lose their dark outline on the near-black slate and read
  as dim brown lumps. Put each icon on a cream `#f4f1dc` disc of r ≈ 55 (tested: it reads at once), or raise them to 0.8. Same rule for
  the icon on any `#0b0f14` death beat.
