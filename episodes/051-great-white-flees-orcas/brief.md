## 7. Risks
- **Gore (highest risk in this topic).** The real story is orcas removing livers.
  - The script states it in one flat sentence and never describes how. No "tore open", no "ripped out", no wound or organ imagery.
  - Carcasses on the beach are clean outlines with X eyes, no marks.
  - The liver is a labelled icon or an X-ray card.
  - No blood in the water anywhere.
  - Doug's deaths are cut to black or a splash with the cap floating.
- **Kid appeal.** Sharks and orcas are a kids' favourite.
  - Keep the tone adult-coded and deadpan: no cute orca faces, no smiling sharks, no "Baby Shark" or "Jaws" references, no nursery voices.
  - No captivity anywhere (021 rule): no captive orcas or names, no theme parks.
- **Title delivery.** "For a year" must be literally sourced (see 1). "Leave" must not suggest the population is extinct or gone forever: item 9's
  honesty line and item 8's both-sides line cover that.
- **Overclaims to avoid:**
  - "Sharks smell dead sharks or orcas and flee" is a hypothesis. Use it only if a paper raises it, hedged as the paper hedges, or cut it.
  - "Orcas are the only reason the sharks left" is contested (item 8 honesty line).
  - Do not name Port and Starboard as the killers of any specific carcass unless the paper does.
  - No exact Farallon shark counts unless they are in the paper.
- **No safety advice:** nothing about swimming, diving or what to do near sharks or orcas. Cage-diving appears only as a setting.
- **Strong sources needed:**
  - Jorgensen et al. 2019 (*Sci. Rep.*) and the Monterey Bay Aquarium release;
  - Towner et al. 2022 (*Afr. J. Mar. Sci.*);
  - Engelbrecht et al. 2019;
  - Hammerschlag et al. 2022;
  - Bowlby et al. 2023 and its rebuttal;
  - Shark Spotters data;
  - a primary or review source for white shark liver mass;
  - Towner et al. 2024 for the Mossel Bay solo kill (callback).
  Never a listicle or a news-aggregator-only source for a number.
- **Repetition:** 008 and 021 already used the headline facts in one line each. The ruling in 0 applies: new detail, new staging, one callback each.
  The screener should fail any item whose twist is a sentence already spoken in 008 or 021.
