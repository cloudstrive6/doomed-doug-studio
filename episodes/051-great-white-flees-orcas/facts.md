## TITLE DELIVERY: "for a year" (brief section 1, required)

**Verdict: supported, with the source's own wording "up to a year".** Monterey Bay Aquarium release on Jorgensen et al. 2019, lead author
Salvador Jorgensen: white sharks "will not return for up to a year, even though the orcas are only passing through." The same release says that in
every case the sharks left the island when orcas arrived and did not return until the following season.
- EurekAlert release (MBA): https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/717413 (Fetched)
- ScienceDaily copy: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2019/04/190416085525.htm (Fetched)
- Paper: Jorgensen, S.J. et al. (2019). "Killer whales redistribute white shark foraging pressure on seals." *Scientific Reports* 9: 6153.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-39356-2 ; PMC6467992 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6467992/ (Abstract via search; PMC blocked by captcha)

The script says "up to a year" (opening, item 6) and states the honesty line that white sharks leave the Farallones every winter anyway (item 6).
The Towner et al. 2022 Gansbaai paper does **not** give a one-year figure for any individual shark that I could retrieve (abstract only), so it
is not used for "a year". Its "2.5 years" figure is used in no spoken line; item 4 uses "weeks, and sometimes months".
