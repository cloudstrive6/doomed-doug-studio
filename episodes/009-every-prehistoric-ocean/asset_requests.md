# Asset requests: 009 One Night in Every Prehistoric Ocean

From the director, for the illustrator (the art director approves). House style follows `assets/library/anglerfish.json`
and `channel/art_bible.md`:
- Creatures, fossils, places and props are drawn in the **detailed tier**: outlines 4-6, flat fills plus `spray` shading, darker back
  and lighter belly, white eyes with black pupils. Everything faces **right** unless stated otherwise. Local coordinates are centred on
  the anchor.
- **Anchors and sizes matter.** I laid out all 337 shots with outline placeholder boxes at exactly the sizes and anchors below and checked
  the contact sheets (counter + night-clock zone, caption bar, crops). `bc` = anchor at the bottom-centre (the lowest point sits on y=0,
  so the drawing spans y -h..0). `c` = anchor at the centre. Sizes are local units at scale 1, width x height. Where a note gives a local
  point (a leg, a whorl, a door), I aimed arrows, rings or overlays at it, so please keep it there.
- **Tone (brief risk list, kid-appeal medium-high):** nature horror for adults. Predators stay at the detailed cartoon tier and look
  dangerous: no cute faces, no smiles, no pastel nursery colours, no sparkly eyes. No blood, no bite wounds, no carcasses; fossils and
  bones at the illustration tier are fine.
- **Dark eras:** the water darkens by era (Cambrian pale blue `#7cc6e6` to Miocene near-black navy `#0a1630`). Livyatan, Megalodon,
  `megalodon_tooth`, `ratfish` and `basilosaurus` must keep mid-tone fills so they read on `#112f4f` / `#0a1630`.
- SIL = also used with `"silhouette": true`, so the outer outline must be clean and recognisable as a black shape.
- Priority: A = hero (8+ shots), B = supporting (3-7), C = one-off.

Reused from the library (no work needed): `time_machine`, `snorkel_gear`, `doug_cap`, `trilobite`, `ammonite`, `conodont`,
