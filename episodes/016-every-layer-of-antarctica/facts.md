# Facts: 016 What Dying Under Every Layer of Antarctica Would Be Like (working)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. These are fiction or framing, not claims: Doug, his deaths, the rope
rig, the suitcase and stickers, selfie, nap, sandwich and fishing rod, clipboard, flag, knocking, the narrator's asides ("does not trust
a cave this comfortable", "nothing at the bottom of a lake has ever ignored Doug before", "the warm patch was warm for a reason"), and the
004 callback ("the last time Doug was in Antarctica, he stood on top of it", which is series lore: 004 Antarctic Plateau).
Everyday comparisons are marked "comparison", with the source for the everyday object's size and the arithmetic.

Checked 2026-10-12 (draft 1). Revised for draft 2 after script_review.md (F1-F5 and fixes 6-8, plus all three advisories). "Verified" means I read the claim on the page (WebFetch). "Search snippet" means I confirmed it from
search-result text that quotes the page, because the page blocked WebFetch or I didn't open it. The screener should re-open those.

**Gore check:** "Blood" appears only in the name, and the twist says "It isn't blood" by about 0:22. No bodies are described. The Mercer
remains are "carcasses ... smaller than a poppy seed", drawn as tiny shells. No injury or hypothermia detail.
**Medical/safety check:** no cold-survival tips. Erebus gas is one line ("the air in volcanic caves is often the dangerous part"), with no
symptoms.
**Kid-appeal check:** no penguins, seals or cute wildlife.
**Climate check:** one flat sourced number for Thwaites (65 cm, attributed to NASA scientists) and one for Denman (5 km retreat). No moral.

**Overstated claims avoided:**
- "Doomsday Glacier" is attributed as a media nickname.
- The Erebus DNA is stated as "not proof that anything is alive".
- The volcano count is from one study, and the region is "potentially one of the largest", in the researchers' own framing.
- Volcano activity increasing with thinning ice is "suggested".
- Denman's depth is an "estimate" from mass conservation, and the script says so.
- Vostok's age is given as a 15 to 25 million year range with "depends on who you ask".
- The Vostok DNA claims (Shtarkman 2013) are disputed, so they are not used.
- The Vostok "50 times oxygen" figure is model-based and contested, so it is not used.
- The fish and sponge food sources are stated as unknown.
- Priscu said finding fish under an ice shelf is "not novel", so the script says "nobody expected" only about the camera moment. The
  sourced superlative is "farthest south fish have ever been found", and the team was reported "startled".
