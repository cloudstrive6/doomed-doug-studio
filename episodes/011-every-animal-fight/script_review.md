### Fact errors (FAIL items)

- **F1. Red Kangaroo, line 121:** "the tail pushes the kangaroo forward harder than its front and back legs combined". The source says *as much as*, not more than. UNSW newsroom: "the tails of red kangaroos provide as much propulsive force as their front and hind legs combined".
- **F2. Grey Wolf, line 155:** "A report from the Norwegian Institute for Nature Research counted four hundred eighty-nine victims". The NINA report abstract says **491** (AGRIS record of NINA Report 1944). 489 comes from the International Wolf Center's summary. The same mismatch exists for the rabid count, 382 vs 380, but that number isn't spoken.
- **F3. Saltwater Crocodile, line 289:** "The biggest factor in who survived was simple: how much bigger the crocodile was than the person." Fukuda 2015 names **two** most influential factors: the body-mass difference and the victim's position relative to the water. Calling one of them "the biggest" and "simple" overclaims.
