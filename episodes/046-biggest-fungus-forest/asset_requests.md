## Item 1: The Corpse Flower

### `rafflesia_flower` (14 shots: s002, s005, s007, s009-s014, s019, s022-s023, s026-s027)
*Rafflesia arnoldii* in full bloom, lying on the forest floor, seen from a low 3/4 view above. It has five thick, fleshy, rounded
petals (lobes) in brick red-brown `#a8402a` covered in raised **cream-white warts** (`#efe0c8` blotches of different sizes). In the
middle is a deep central cup (the diaphragm) with a round dark opening `#3a1410` and a ring of small pale spikes around the rim.
The flower is heavy, rubbery and slightly ugly, with no stem and no leaves. Anchor **centre**, about 620 x 300 (x -310..310,
y -150..150). It is used at 0.35-1.1, so it must still read as "giant warty red flower" at 0.35, where it sits on Doug's vine
costume at belly height. No flies are drawn into it (they are placed separately).

about 200 x 180 (x -100..100, y -180..0). Used at 0.45-0.9.

### `tetrastigma_vine` (s015, s018-s019, s022-s023, s027)

leaflets) on short stalks along the top. Anchor **centre**, about 1400 x 160 (x -700..700, y -80..80). The stem's top surface sits
about local y -30 (a bud grows there). Used at 0.8-1.3.

### `vine_cells_diagram` (s015-s017)

green-walled plant cells (pale green `#cfe6b0` fill, darker walls). Thin **purple-pink `#b05a8a` threads** (the Rafflesia strands)
weave between and through the cells, so you can't tell which cells belong to whom. No text. Anchor **centre**, 520 x 520.
