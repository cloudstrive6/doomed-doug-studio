# Art review: 012 What Dying in Every Natural Trap Would Be Like

**Scope:** the 33 new drawings in `asset_requests.md` (previews `assets/previews/012-assets-*.png`). The grid sheets crop the
tall drawings (`snowy_fir`, `brinicle`, `eight_story_building`, `ancient_conifer_trunk`), so I also re-rendered every asset at
scale 1 on a large canvas and measured its bounding box against the requested box. I checked the keypoints the director aims
at by sampling pixels: the Dead Sea sinkhole ring, the Italy dots for Altamura and the Dolomites, the Valais dot, and the brinicle
taper. I rendered about 70 context keyframes covering every new asset, every death beat, the real-victim cards and the three STAY cards.
I then ruled on the four art-director notes.

## Verdict: PASS

All 33 drawings are approved. None goes back to the illustrator. I made one change to the shotlist myself (note 2, the brinicle
background colour). There is one small composition note for the director, listed at the end.

## Rulings on the art-director notes

1. **Capless upright Doug in the amber bead (s291-s293): APPROVED as a logged one-off.** The script's image is the cap sitting outside
   the bead while Doug is preserved inside. That is the same kind of exception as 008's orca wearing the cap. The cap stays visible in
   every shot, which satisfies style bible 7.2. Doug keeps `ink: "#000000"` on the light bead. **Also approved:** these shots tick the
   counter with a living expression (`hopeful`, mid-wave) and without `on_back`. "Preserved mid-wave" is the scripted death image, and
   the narration says "Doug has died. Again." This is a named variant, not a precedent for normal deaths, which still need `dead` X eyes
   or a cap-only image. Logged in `decisions.md` and the art bible.
2. **Brinicle background colour: CHANGED to `#3a9ad9`.** The art bible rule says that water under sea ice is the surface colour, and it
   applies to every episode, whatever the axis. The ramp exists so that every ocean video on the channel uses the same colour language. A
   returning viewer reads `#1f3a5f`, which sits between our twilight and midnight colours, as "deep" or "night". A brinicle forms in
   shallow water a few metres under the ice. "Antarctic" comes across through the white ice ceiling, the grey seabed and the icicle, not
   through dark water. The brief left the ramp to me, and the sequence still steps clearly (red-orange canyon, then bright under-ice
   blue, then grey avalanche). I edited `shotlist.json` in all 31 brinicle shots (s061-s091): background `#1f3a5f` to `#3a9ad9`, and
   the water speckle spray `#2c4d78` to `#6fc1ee` (the 008 surface-water speckle). I re-rendered s061-s091. Doug auto-inks black, and the
   brinicle, seal, sea star, urchin, costume, wordart and the white ice web all still read. **Director: change your generator's `NAVY`
   constant to `#3a9ad9` too, so a later regeneration does not revert this.**
3. **Death beats and the cap: CONFIRMED.** Every death shows the red cap: the tree well (cap beside the ski tips), the canyon,
   avalanche and glacier (cap only), the crevasse (ghost with cap), the tar pit (buried-Doug rule with `dead` X eyes plus ghost), Altamura
   (cap on `rock_lump`) and amber (cap on the bead). Not using `on_back` is correct, because none of these shows a lying body. I checked
   s030, s057-s058, s116-s117, s180-s181, s215, s248-s249 and s291-s293.
4. **Real victims: CONFIRMED.** s047-s051 and s169-s175 are muted dark cards and diagrams with no Doug, no people and no jokes. Altamura
   never shows a skeleton.

I checked notes 5 and 6 as well (the Dead Sea kicker and the STAY cards). They are fine as built. The STAY tags stay optional, and I am
not asking for them.
