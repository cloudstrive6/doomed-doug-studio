# Asset requests: 012 What Dying in Every Natural Trap Would Be Like

From the director, for the illustrator (the art director approves). House style: `assets/library/anglerfish.json` and
`channel/art_bible.md`.
- **Detailed tier** for creatures, props and places: outlines 4-6, flat fills plus `spray` shading, a darker back and a lighter belly,
  white eyes with black pupils. Everything faces **right** unless a note says otherwise. Local coordinates are centred on the anchor.
- **Anchors and sizes.** I laid out all 298 shots with placeholder boxes at exactly the sizes and anchors below, then checked the
  contact sheets for the counter zone, caption bar, crops and overlaps. `bc` means the anchor is at the bottom-centre: the lowest point
  sits on y=0 and the drawing spans y -h..0. `c` means the anchor is at the centre. Sizes are local units at scale 1, width x height.
  Please stay inside the bbox I give. Where a note gives a local point (a seat, a map dot, a wound), I aimed arrows or dots at it.
- **Tone.** Adult-coded nature horror (brief, section 7). The kid-appeal risk sits with the sea-star and mite costumes and the amber bead:
  no smiles, no blush, no sparkly eyes, no pastel nursery colours. **No blood, wounds, bones of people or remains on any drawing.**
  Trapped animals (bison, wolves, sea stars) look stuck or annoyed, never hurt.
- **Priority:** A = hero (8+ shots), B = supporting (3-7), C = one-off.

## Notes for the art director (not illustrator work)
1. **Capless upright Doug inside the amber bead, s291-s293.** The script and brief need the red cap sitting *on top of* the bead,
   outside it, while Doug is inside mid-wave. Doug uses `pose: "wave"` with `gear: ["cap_off"]` and `doug_cap` sits on the bead top.
   The art bible lists capless upright Doug as an approved one-off only, so please log it in `decisions.md` if you approve it.
2. **Brinicle background colour.** The brief and the script both call for an "Antarctic navy" scene, and the brief's ramp puts it
   after the canyon. I used `#1f3a5f`, which is not one of the ocean-ramp colours. The art bible's rule that water under sea ice stays
   `#3a9ad9` was written for depth-axis ocean episodes. This episode's axis is time, not depth. If you want the rule applied here anyway,
   it is one constant (`NAVY`) in my generator, so it is a quick swap.
3. **Death beats and the cap.** Every death shows the red cap: the tree well puts the cap on the snow beside the ski tips (s030-s031). The
   canyon, avalanche and glacier have the cap alone, as scripted. The crevasse death beat (s180) uses ghost Doug, with his cap, rising
   from the refilled hole before the "75 YEARS LATER" card. The tar pit has Doug stuck to the hips (buried-Doug rule: the surface rect sits
   exactly at hip y) with `dead` X eyes plus a ghost. Altamura has the cap on `rock_lump`. Amber has the cap on the bead. None of these
   use `on_back`, because the script's death images are ski tips, a cap, a lump and a bead. Please confirm.
4. **Real victims (CD condition).** s047-s051 (Antelope Canyon 1997) and s169-s175 (Tsanfleuron 2017, Valais) are plain dark caption
   cards with muted diagrams, no Doug, no people, no jokes. Altamura never shows a skeleton: `rock_lump` and a `head_outline` with a
   red circle on the nose only.
5. **Dead Sea kicker (CD note).** s147-s148 are the strong visual for the flat line: the sinkhole map fills with a second wave of 48
   yellow-orange dots popping in across the shot, a year label ticking 2011 to 2015, "400 A YEAR" wordart and a slow push-in. The editor
   should cut straight to the LONG STAY card (s149, hold 0.2) with no extra hold on s148.
6. **STAY section cards** (s006 SHORT STAY, s149 LONG STAY, s218 PERMANENT STAY) are hotel-style: a white sign with red title text and a
   brass `house_key`, and Doug waving with the suitcase. The caption bar is hidden on these three and on the closing suitcase shots
   s294-s298. I did not add the optional per-item STAY tags. If you want them, the CD-approved values are in the script header.
