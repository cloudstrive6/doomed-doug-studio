**Fact error:**
- **F1, Dead Sea kicker (line 175).** "the holes keep coming, at a rate of around four hundred new ones every year." This is NBC's 2015 figure ("are now reported... every year") stated as a current rate. That is the same problem as last round's F2. Newer mapping does not support 400 a year now: the western shore had 702 new sinkholes from 2005 to 2021, about 44 a year (Unizar/Gutiérrez et al. 2023), and Ghor Al-Haditha had about 500 from 2018 to 2022 (EGU23-13530). The current rate is unsourced and probably lower.

**Advisory:** the 18-minute Swiss curve (line 135) is correctly told as one study's result. Note that a 2024 JAMA Network Open update (Brugger co-author) shortens the 90%-survival phase to about 10 minutes. The past tense keeps line 135 true; do not make it present tense.
