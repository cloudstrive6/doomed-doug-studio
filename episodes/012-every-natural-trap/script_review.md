**Fact errors (each one must be fixed):**
- **F1, opener (line 7).** "Most hold you for minutes." This is false by the script's own STAY tags: only 2 of 9 traps (tree well, slot canyon) work in minutes. The brief's draft line was "Some hold you for minutes."
- **F2, Dead Sea (line 157).** "Since nineteen eighty its surface has dropped by about thirty meters". This is the 2015 NBC figure stated in the present tense. The level was about 400 m below sea level in the early 1980s and about 439.8 m below in 2025, so the drop is now about 40 m.
- **F3, Dead Sea (line 159).** "One geologist with the Geological Survey of Israel puts the share... at about thirty percent". NBC 2015 gives the 30% as the reporters' own unattributed statement. Gidi Baer is quoted only on the level drop. This is a misattribution.
- **F4, Dead Sea (line 165).** "As many as five thousand sinkholes". "As many as" presents an old floor as a ceiling. Israel's Ministry of Environmental Protection counted about 5,500 by 2015, and the 2019 Solid Earth preprint counts more than 6,000. facts.md itself calls 5,000 "a floor".
- **F5, Tar pit (line 235).** "the dire wolf, with more than four thousand individuals". NHM LA says "topping 4,000 **specimens**". Specimens are not individuals, and the cited count PDF was never read.
- **F6, Amber (line 307).** "the bigger one is only about twice as wide as a human hair". The source (Live Science) says *Triasacarus* is 210 microns **long**, about twice a hair's **diameter**. The script gets the dimension wrong; the mite is a thin worm shape.
- **F7, Altamura (line 275).** "he is the oldest Neanderthal anyone has ever pulled genetic material from". This was Lari's claim in 2015. It is contradicted by Sima de los Huesos: nuclear DNA from fossils of about 430,000 years, published in Nature in 2016 (Meyer et al.), with the population classed as early Neanderthals (Max Planck press release).
