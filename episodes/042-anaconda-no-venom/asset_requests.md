# 042 Anaconda: No Venom: asset requests (director, 2026-11-09)

39 new drawings. House style as usual: creatures and props in the detailed tier (outline 4-6, flat fills, interior shading,
texture strokes 2-3), humans in the crude stick tier (`auto_ink`). Nothing cute: no big shiny eyes, no smiles on animals.
No blood, no wounds and no gore anywhere (scars = a few pale healed lines only). Sizes are local units at scale 1.
"Anchor bottom-centre" means feet, belly or wheels sit on y=0. The shotlist already uses these names, anchors and rough sizes,
so keep them. If one has to change, tell the director. Episode backdrops run from bright swamp day (`#cfe9ff` / water `#6f8a6e`)
down to near-black night (`#0b1020` / `#0d1410`), so every creature needs a mid-tone fill that reads on both.

**Green anaconda palette (use it on every anaconda drawing so they all match):** olive-green body `#5f7a2e`, darker back `#4a5f22`,
pale yellow-olive belly scutes `#c9c06a`, big round black oval spots `#1a1a12` along the back, smaller black spots with a yellowish
centre along the flanks, and an orange-tan stripe edged in black running back from the eye. Small head, barely wider than the neck,
eyes and nostrils on TOP of the head. Small mean eye, round pupil, no fangs.
