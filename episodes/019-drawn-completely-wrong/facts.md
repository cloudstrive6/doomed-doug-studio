# Facts: 019 The Scariest Prehistoric Animals Scientists Drew Completely Wrong (working)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Fiction and framing are not claims: Doug, his deaths and
the counter, the time machine, the OLD DRAWING cards and their red X stamps, the fencing mask, shield, ladder, family photo,
water-plant gift, dive mask, speech bubbles, the giant salamander walking past, the Iguanodon's thumbs up, the narrator's
asides ("the feet were drawn correctly", "Doug was not considered for the name", "too strange to eat", "first fossil of
Doug", "the tree is fine", "the narrator will draw Doug's version"). Everyday comparisons are marked "comparison", with the
source for the everyday object and the arithmetic.

Checked 2026-10-15 (draft 1); revised for draft 2 after script_review.md (fixes 1-7 and recommendations). "Verified" = I read the claim on the page (WebFetch). "Search snippet" = confirmed from
search-result text quoting the page, because the page blocked WebFetch (403/503) or was not opened. The screener should
re-open those.

**Gore check:** no injury descriptions. The Allosaurus bone is described only as a missing piece and a hole that a spike
fits. No claw, spike or tooth touches Doug in the narration; every death cuts to dust, splash, mud or falling leaves.
**Kid-appeal check:** "dinosaur" is never in a spoken joke about cuteness; no babies, no nicknames.
**Mockery check:** Scheuchzer's reading is framed as "a natural reading for a scholar of his time"; no religion joke.
Maleev, Marsh, Mantell and Cope are presented as working from fragments. The Cope/Marsh feud is presented as history.
**Overstated claims avoided:** Brontosaurus is not said to have "never existed"; the 2015 revival is given with "not
everyone agrees" (NBC, Holtz). Iguanodon's thumb use is open (defense or feeding). Stegosaurus plate pattern: "not every
drawing agrees". Spinosaurus: no "bigger than T. rex"; "one of the longest predatory dinosaurs"; swim vs wade presented
with no winner; the species question is flagged. Dimetrodon is called a dinosaur only as the error. No "second brain".
