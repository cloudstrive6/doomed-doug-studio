## Fixed by the art director (done, re-previewed)
1. **`spotted_hyena_cap`**: the cap hung below the jaw line, so at feed size it read as a red tongue (or worse, blood)
   rather than Doug's cap. Moved the cap group so the dome sits up inside the jaws, teeth gripping it and brim forward
   (group x 112→92, y 60→42, scale 0.45→0.52, rotate 8→0; ~100 units wide). Now reads as "cap in mouth" at 1.2, 0.6 and 0.3.
2. **`field_guide_lion_hyena`**: "SPOTTED HYENA" ran into the top-right dog-ear (the A was half covered). Size 25→22,
   x 140→130; now clear of the dog-ear and still inside x +20..+265. MAYBE and NO stamp zones unchanged and clear.
3. **`carcass_outline`**: the horn was a thin hook that read as a pig's tail, so the shape looked like a sleeping pig or
   hippo. Thickened it (outline 10→16, fill 3→8) and reshaped it as an upward horn. Still outline-only, no interior detail.
   Reads on sunset, night and near-black grounds.
