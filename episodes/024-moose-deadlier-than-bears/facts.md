## Brief leads that failed or changed
- **Grizzly as fast as a moose (The Run):** fails. ADF&G gives brown bears up to 64 km/h; moose about 56 km/h. The script says the bear is faster on open ground and moves the twist to terrain.
- **Moose crossing bogs (The Run):** no source on bogs or gait in bogs. Replaced with NPS: deep snow, deep water, obstacles (fallen timber).
- **Dive depth "up to about 6 m" (The Dive):** only blogs and a secondary Yukon page; no primary source. Replaced with Peterson's 1955 report of dives of more than 18 ft, worded as "reported" with "he never said how he measured it" (round 1, F2).
- **Bears and fermented fruit (The Apples):** the only source (Atlas Obscura) says drunk-bear stories are anecdotal and could not be loaded; cut. The tie is on raiding fruit trees (Idaho Fish and Game).
- **"A bear fights with its mouth" (The Hooves)** and **"a bear keeps the same teeth" (The Rut):** unsourced; cut.
- **Windshield-height mechanism (The Highway):** only weak sources, and it edges toward injury description; cut. The Maine odds ratio carries "far more dangerous".
- **Newfoundland "no grizzlies":** no direct statement found; the script uses the government's "largest land carnivore" wording for the black bear.
- **Newfoundland 1878 count (two):** not in the government source; no number spoken.
- **Newfoundland human deaths per year:** no official current figure found. The script uses the 1987-88 peer-reviewed count and the 2012-2020 collision average only.
- **Alaska moose-vehicle deaths:** a 2000-2012 DOT figure (23 deaths) appears only second-hand; not used.
- **ADF&G deer-collision fatality rate for Maine (0.02%):** not in the abstract we could read; replaced with the odds ratio.
- **Over-claiming check for the titler:** the sources support "moose injure more people in Alaska than bears each year" (ADF&G) and "a moose crash is far deadlier than a deer crash" (Maine). They do **not** show that moose kill more people than bears overall: Alaska bear deaths run zero to two a year, and the moose road-death figures we found are of the same order. Keep "to Doug" in any "Deadlier" title (screener round 1). The title's "deadlier to Doug" is literally true in the episode (8 to 0).
