## Advisory (do not block)

- **A1. Pangaea Splits repeats the Siberian twist.**
  - The Siberian kicker is "set off by magma that never reached the surface". The new paragraph opens with "Some of
    the magma never reached the surface."
  - A viewer will hear this as a rerun, not a callback.
  - Option 1: make it a deliberate, flat callback, for example "Some of the magma never reached the surface, just like
    in Siberia."
  - Option 2: open the paragraph with the Brazil fact instead.
  - Either way, keep the item at 290 words or fewer.
- **A2. "rock rich in oil and ancient remains":** "ancient remains" can sound like fossils or bones. The source means
  organic matter. Consider "rock rich in oil and buried organic matter", which is the same length.
- **A3. Killer Moss kicker:** "a plant with no real roots and no flowers" restates the frame paragraph. Optional: use
  a new stark fact of the same length. It must not be a joke, because rule 10 is at its limit in this item.
- **A4. facts.md, Ozone Hole:** the supernova note still quotes the old framing ("another team proposed ... may have
  done it"). Update it to the draft 3 wording.
- **A5. Art (director and visual screener):**
  - Hot Tub: draw the steam rising off the water, not off Doug.
  - Ozone Hole sunburn: keep Doug's locked design. Show the burn as a colour fill and cartoon peel flakes on the nose,
    with no skin detail.
- **A6. Item length ceilings:** Pangaea Splits (290), Killer Moss (280) and Chicxulub (377) are at or near their
  ceilings. A polish pass must not add words to these three items.
