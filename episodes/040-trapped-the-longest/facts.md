# Facts: 040 The Most Horrifying Things Nature Kept Trapped the Longest (draft 1)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Checked 2026-11-08.

How each source was checked:
- **Fetched** means I read the passage on the page or in the paper (WebFetch, or Europe PMC full text).
- **Search** means I confirmed the claim only from search-result text quoting the page (page blocked or behind a wall).
- **Arithmetic** means a conversion or comparison worked out from sourced numbers. The working is shown.

The screener should re-open the **Search** rows and the FLAGS first.

## FLAGS for the screener
1. **Order change (Mammoth Sinkhole).** The brief's 26,000-year date is superseded. Mahan, Mead, Hanson, Krolczyk & Jass, *Quaternary Research*, published 11 August 2026 (doi 10.1017/qua.2026.10102), IRSL on feldspar from the bone bed: about 232,000 to 160,000 years. Confirmed by the Mammoth Site's own release, Wikipedia and Cowboy State Daily. I could not open the paper itself. A USGS data release of 2016-19 OSL data gives about 130-255 ka by a different method; the script uses only the paper's IRSL range. So the sinkhole is now item 7, after the worms (46 ka) and virus (48.5 ka), under a new band "Hundreds of thousands of years."
2. **Ötzi preservation is contested.** Pilø et al. 2022 (*The Holocene*, "Ötzi, 30 years on") argue it is unlikely a glacier moved over the site; the South Tyrol museum's conservator disagrees. The script says only that ice sat on him in the gully and that "scientists still argue about exactly how the ice behaved". The brief's "the glacier flowed over him and missed" twist and the rug analogy are cut.
3. **Nutty Putty distances** conflict (Wikipedia 400 ft from the entrance; Deseret News 700 ft inside and 150 ft down). None are spoken. "More than a hundred volunteers" is the family's "100-plus volunteers" (Deseret News).
4. **Nutty Putty sealing material.** KSL says "explosives and cement"; Wikipedia says entry points were filled with concrete. The script uses KSL's wording.
5. **Snowbound car: two different doctors.** The igloo line is Dr Ulf Segerberg (chief medical officer, Norrland University Hospital; Irish Times). The hibernation idea and the 31 °C estimate are Dr Stefan Branth (NBC News, quoting Västerbottens-Kuriren). The script says "the hospital's chief doctor" and "Another doctor suggested". No inside-the-car temperature was found, so none is spoken.
6. **Worm dating has a critic.** Byron Adams (BYU) told Scientific American the authors had not ruled out surface contamination; co-author Kurzchalia defends the sterile procedures. The script says this in one line.
7. **Hoyo Negro chamber width** (61 m) is from sci.news's summary of Chatters 2014, not the paper text (paywalled). La Nación reports 62 m from later 3D mapping. Depth figures conflict (40 m below sea level for the remains; 55-60 m for the pit in other sources), so no fall height is spoken.
8. **Seafloor oxygen.** The brief said "almost no oxygen"; the sources say the opposite (oxygen was present in all cores; the paper's title is about *oxic* sediment). The script says oxygen seeps all the way down.
9. **"Energy millions of times lower"** is Morono's quoted statement as reported in search-result text; I did not fetch the page that carries the quote.
