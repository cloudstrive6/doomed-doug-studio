# Facts: 031 Why the Polar Bear Is the Scariest Animal to Be Hunted By (working, draft 2)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Checked 2026-10-29 (draft 1); updated for draft 2 per script_review.md (fixes 1-3, Swim trim).

How each source was checked:
- **Read** means I downloaded the primary text and read the passage in full. These are Gjertz & Lydersen 1986 (the full PDF, text extracted), the Wilder et al. 2017 USGS abstract, the Pagano et al. 2024 USGS abstract and the Nature Communications full text, and Manitoba's two Polar Bear Alert PDFs (text extracted).
- **Fetched** means I read the claim on the web page with WebFetch.
- **Search** means I confirmed the claim only from search-result text quoting the page. In these cases the page blocked fetching or I did not open it.

The screener should re-open the "Search" lines first, and the **FLAG** lines.

**FLAGGED for sourcing (screener decision needed):**
1. **Churchill bears get "no food"** (The Town, and the closing kicker). Screener ruling A (draft 1): accepted with a fix. No Manitoba government page I could reach states it. The sources are Churchill Wild, a Churchill tour operator ("The bears are provided with water or snow, but are not fed"), and HuffPost UK, 19 Dec 2011, the article's own statement; it quotes the Born Free Foundation, not facility staff ("Snow is pushed through the cages, but they get no food"). Nothing contradicts it. Draft 2 attributes it as "Reports from Churchill say", not to facility staff. The Wikipedia citation is dropped.
2. **Wilder et al.'s period comparison** comes from Hakai Magazine's report of the paper, not the paper itself (paywalled and behind Cloudflare). The abstract's numbers are Read.
3. **"Through a meter of snow / more than a kilometer away"** is from SeaWorld's education page ("most likely"). Polar Bears International pages could not be fetched (captcha). Derocher (University of Alberta, via the Naked Scientists) says the range has never been tested in a controlled experiment, so the script says "probably".

**Cut from the brief because no adequate source was found:** "one of the few animals on Earth that will hunt a person as food" (fallback used); the 32 km / 20 mile range (banned anyway); "hollow" hairs (tour-operator sources only); Koon 1998 as a *Nature* paper (the venue could not be confirmed, so no journal is named); Øritsland / Best thermal figures; "freezes whenever the seal lifts its head" (one uncited blog); the nose-covering myth (no source either way); an aquatic-stalk success rate (none found; the record dive's miss is used instead); paw papillae; Churchill cell total and holding time (sources conflict: 20, 28, "about 50" cells; 2 to 30 days, "minimum of 30 days"); Wilder's definition of "predatory" (not reachable). Note: Manitoba's Polar Bear Protection Regulation (M.R. 210/2002) requires a balanced diet for bears kept in *permitted facilities* (zoos). It is not used and must not be cited for the holding facility.
