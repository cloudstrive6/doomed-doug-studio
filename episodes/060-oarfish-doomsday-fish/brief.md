## 7. Risks
- **Gore.** No cut flesh at the autotomy: the missing half is a clean cartoon stump with a healed rounded end, no red. No necropsy or dissection
  drawings, no knives. Parasites are cartoon ribbons, no segments, no worms leaving a body, no human infection claims. Deaths are cut-aways.
- **Kid appeal (medium-high).** Octonauts has an oarfish kids' episode (191K, signals), so a "cute sea serpent" read is a made-for-kids signal.
  The oarfish is drawn long, silver and blank-eyed, never smiling; humour stays adult-coded and dry. Made-for-kids No.
- **Sensitive topics.** Earthquakes killed real people: no quake imagery, no Tōhoku footage style, no "the fish warned them". The belief is
  attributed and respectful; the twist is that a study found no link. Never present it as a mocked superstition.
- **Misleading packaging.** The fish does not predict earthquakes, is not dangerous to people, and is not "over 11 m" (026 rule: conservative 8 m,
  Guinness 7.6 m specimen). The title's self-amputation claim must match the facts.md wording.
- **Strong sources needed:** Orihara et al. 2019 (BSSA), Benfield et al. 2013 (*J. Fish Biol.*), Roberts 2012 (or a museum page) for autotomy and
  no regrowth, Scripps Institution of Oceanography 2024 strandings releases, CIMI / USC reporting on the 2013 Catalina fish and its parasites,
  FishBase / Australian Museum for anatomy and diet, a source for "king of herrings" and *Regalecus*. Never a competitor video or listicle.
