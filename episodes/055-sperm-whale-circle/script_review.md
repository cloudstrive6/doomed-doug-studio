## Targeted checks
- **"In one well-studied Caribbean population ... around forty minutes ... around five hundred meters": OK.** PMC3774244 places the study population off Dominica, Eastern Caribbean. It says "babysitters for calves at the surface while mothers make long (approx. 40 min), deep (approx. 500 m) foraging dives". The script's label is correct.
  - **facts.md citation error (not a script fix):** facts.md lines 62–63 cite the paper as "*R. Soc. Open Sci.*". The correct citation is Gero, Gordon & Whitehead 2013, "Calves as social hubs: dynamics of the social network within sperm whale units", *Proc. R. Soc. B* 280: 20131113. (*R. Soc. Open Sci.* did not exist until 2014.)
- **Arnbom et al. 1987: citation OK.** *J. Mammalogy* 68(2):450-453, killer whales, off the Galápagos, west of Isabela, early 1985. The 31 whales and the "tight flotilla" match Pitman p. 504, and the "minor injuries ... ultimately successful in warding off the attackers" wording matches Pitman's Introduction. Calling it a "tight raft" and not a ring is correct.
- **F1. Arnbom outcome: FAIL (contradicted).** The script says "the orcas left without a kill". Arnbom's own title text describes "the subsequent flight of the **sperm whales** from the area of the attack". Pitman's Discussion also cites Arnbom for sperm whales that "flee from an area after an encounter with predators". So the sperm whales left. Nothing says the orcas did.
- Also spot-rechecked against the Pitman text: "assumed they escape by diving" (Rice 1989), "no attempt to escape by diving" (p. 260 of the OCR text), the rescue sequence, and Berzin. All OK.
