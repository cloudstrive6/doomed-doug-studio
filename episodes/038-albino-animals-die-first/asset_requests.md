## Rules for every drawing in this episode (CD notes, brief section 7)
- **Kid-appeal risk is HIGH.** White baby animals are a children's favourite, so: **no big cute eyes, no eye shine, no smiles, no blush, no pastel colours.** Eyes are small, set under a heavy brow, and the animals look unimpressed. Hatchlings are drawn in the channel's plain detailed style, the same as `croc_hatchling` ("Not cute: no big eyes").
- **Real white is warm and shaded, not pure flat white.** Use an off-white base (`#f2efe6` / `#f1ebdc`) with grey or tan shading (`#cfcac0`, `#d8cfbb`) and clear dark outlines, so each animal reads on cream (`#f4f1dc`), yellow savanna (`#d9b45a`), dark forest (`#10200f`), night sand (`#5a4b3d`) and the near-black swamp (`#0f1e1c`).
- **Albino animals have pink or red eyes and pinkish skin** (nose, ears, inner ears, feet). The exception is Snowflake, whose eyes are **pale blue**. **Leucistic animals keep normal dark eyes:** golden for the lioness, dark brown for the deer, dark for the duck.
- **No gore.** No wounds, no blood, no lesions, no skin damage on Snowflake. Sunburn appears **only as a colour change on the costume** (white, pink, red), never on Doug's skin and never on an animal.
- Costumes are worn props: anchor = Doug's hips, listed after Doug at the same x/y/scale. **Doug's head, face and red cap stay fully visible** (art bible: costumes). The cap always sits on top.
