### Thumb C (`thumbnail_c.png`, grid): FAIL
CD fix 1 (death pressure in every panel) is done: a hard sun with rays over the gorilla, a dark lion silhouette with a yellow eye in the
grass behind the lioness, and a black cat with a green eye looming over the squirrel. All three read at feed size. CD fix 3 (squirrel pose)
is done: it is now a side-on crouch with a pink eye, and the begging paws are gone. The gorilla is where it fails.

Required fixes:
1. **Gorilla panel, people-read (graphic designer, `thumbnail_test_c.json`):** the new face is ape-like (brow ridge, wide nostrils,
   muzzle), but the white body has a hood rim around the face, seam lines down the body, tubular mitten-like pink forearms and straight
   legs. As a result, the whole figure reads as **a dark-faced person in a white hooded snowsuit**. At 168x94 it is a white suit with a dark face
   in the hood. That is the exact people-read the brief forbids, and a dark face framed in a white costume carries an extra
   bad-optics risk. Redraw the body as an ape: knuckle-walking or a hunched sit with long arms reaching the ground, hands as knuckles, no hood edge
   around the face (let the fur run into the head with a sagittal crest bump), no vertical seam strokes, and a shaggy fur edge instead of
   clean suit outlines.
2. **Gorilla face colour, fact error (graphic designer):** the face is now solid dark brown. Snowflake was albino, with "white hair, pink
   skin, blue eyes" (facts.md, Prado-Martinez 2013), and the episode's whole thesis is *no melanin*. Use a pink/flesh face, and get the ape read
   from shape (heavy brow shelf, wide flat nose with big nostrils, protruding muzzle, small deep-set pale-blue eyes, darker pink shading
   only at the edges), not from dark skin. Keep the eyebrow strokes off.
3. **Squirrel species read (graphic designer, advisory, strongly recommended with the redraw):** the side-on crouch fixed the cute
   read, but the round ears, pointed pink snout, whiskers and smooth-edged tail now make it read as a **white lab rat** at both sizes. Make the
   tail shaggy and fluffy (jagged fur edge, wider at the tip), add small pointed ear tufts and shorten the snout.
4. Optional, still open from the CD gate: the purple field plus a classic rayed cartoon sun is the most nursery-looking corner of the
   package. Mute the purple toward the night/swamp darks and draw the sun as a hard white-yellow glare disc without stick rays.

The lioness and gator/Doug panels pass. C stays out of the A/B/C test until fixes 1 and 2 are re-checked.

**Round summary: A PASS, B PASS, C FAIL.** Packaging can proceed with A as `thumbnail.png` and B as the test variant. C needs the
gorilla redraw (and ideally the squirrel tail) plus a re-check.
