## Required fixes
**R1 (Facts, rule 11).** "...boats must stay at least five hundred meters away from him, and aircraft six hundred and ten." facts.md has no source for the aircraft figure. The facts.md row and the Queensland page it cites cover vessels (500 m) only. I re-fetched that page, and it does not mention aircraft. The figure is true: a Queensland Government ministerial statement (statements.qld.gov.au/statements/85169, 30 July 2018) says aircraft, drones included, may not come closer than 610 m to white whales such as Migaloo. Older statements give 2,000 ft, which is about 610 m. **Fix: add that source to the item 2 row in facts.md.** The other option is to cut ", and aircraft six hundred and ten" (8 words, which still leaves 1,991).

**R2 (H2 analogy: number mismatch).** Narration: "They checked about two and a half billion letters of his genome." The label reads "2.6 BILLION LETTERS", and the "more than eighty years" maths only works from the paper's 2,587 Mbp. Two and a half billion seconds is 79 years, which is under eighty. **Fix: change the narration to "about two point six billion letters"** so it matches the label and the maths. The other option is to keep "two and a half" and change the label to "2.5 BILLION" and the line to "about eighty years".
