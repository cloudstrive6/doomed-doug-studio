- **Albinism vs leucism is the spine of the "why"** and must be explained once, early (item 1 or 3): albinism = no melanin at all, pink or red eyes and

  This is what makes the title true: it is the **eyes** and the **sun** that kill albinos, not the colour alone.

### Decades.

**1. The White Gorilla** (hook item, one of the three strongest; DECADES; death 295)
- Facts:
  - Snowflake (Copito de Nieve), the only known white gorilla, a western lowland gorilla at Barcelona Zoo, captured as an infant in Equatorial Guinea
    in 1966; died in 2003 of skin cancer, at about 40 (verify age and cause).
  - His genome (Prado-Martinez et al. 2013, *BMC Genomics*) traced the albinism to a mutation in the gene **SLC45A2** and showed his parents were
    most likely **uncle and niece** (verify wording: "close relatives, likely uncle and niece").
  - Albinism means no melanin, and melanin is the body's own sunscreen (sourced general fact; any vet/biology source).
- Analogy: forty years in the shade of a zoo is about as long as a typical car stays on the road three times over (writer picks a sourced, honest one,
  or uses "longer than most people keep a job" only if it holds; otherwise a simpler time analogy).
- **Cold-open fact (first sentence):** the death, with the number, e.g. "The only white gorilla ever found lived forty years, and the sun killed him anyway."
  (Adjust to what the source supports: "skin cancer" is the cause; "the sun" only if a source links it to UV exposure. If not, say "skin cancer".)
- **Twist by 0:45 (≤145 words):** the reason he was white was inside his own family tree (uncle and niece). The rarest gorilla in the world was a
  family accident, and he only made it to forty because a zoo kept him out of the forest.
- **Honesty line, one sentence:** his capture is stated plainly and without detail (no account of his family group's deaths; skip it entirely if the
  writer can't say it in one neutral clause).
- Doug beat: Doug in a white gorilla suit, sitting in full sun "to keep Snowflake's memory alive". "Doug did not agree to this." (here; he was volunteered).

**3. The White Lion** (DECADES; strong mid-item, the myth-buster; death 296)
- Facts:
  - The white lions of Timbavati (South Africa) are **leucistic**, not albino: a recessive pigment-gene variant (a **TYR** variant has been reported for white lions;
    **verify the gene and paper**, and don't confuse it with the white-tiger SLC45A2 paper, Xu et al. 2013). They have normal-coloured eyes.
  - A field study found white lions in the wild hunt as successfully as tawny ones (Turner et al. 2015, *African Journal of Ecology*, verify).
  - So the white coat alone didn't doom them. The difference is the eyes: true albino mammals have reduced vision (underdeveloped fovea, misrouted
    optic nerve fibres; Jeffery 1997, *Survey of Ophthalmology*, "The albino retina"; verify).
- Analogy: a white lion stands out on dry grass like a sheet of paper on a brown carpet, and still eats as often as the others.
- Twist: being white isn't what kills you. Being white **and unable to see** is. Every true albino in the rest of this video has both problems.
- Doug beat: no costume needed. Doug, tiny, walks up to a white lioness to compliment the coat. "hi friend?" (the only one this episode). Her eyes work
  perfectly. Cut to black. Death 296, flat. The cap sits in the grass. Field guide: "WHITE LION: LEUCISTIC (EYES FINE)".

**6. The White Bird** (YEARS; death 299; midpoint strong item)

    leucistic (van Grouw 2006, *Dutch Birding*, "Not every white bird is an albino"; and/or 2013 *British Birds*; verify the wording).
  - One concrete documented case to anchor it (an albino bird found in the wild and its fate) only if sourced; otherwise skip.
- Analogy: flight feathers that wear down like a pair of white sneakers on gravel (honest: an image, not a number).
- Twist: an albino bird is attacked from both sides: it can't see the hawk coming, and if it tries to flee, its wings are wearing out under it.

- **People with albinism (HIGH sensitivity, the main risk of this episode):**

  - Banned words: freak, mutant, abnormal, defective, cursed, "pure white" mystique, any race or skin-colour framing. "Born white" only about animals.

- **Gore (keep it cartoon):** sunburn is a colour change on the **costume** only, never on skin; no lesions, no tumours, no medical images for Snowflake.
  Predation is off screen behind a cut to black. No hunting, guns or carcasses (item 4). No dead hatchlings shown (items 7-8), only the cap.

  detailed cartoon style, pink-eyed where albino, unsmiling. Jokes stay adult-coded and dry (town ordinances, the uncle-and-niece genome, the ranger
