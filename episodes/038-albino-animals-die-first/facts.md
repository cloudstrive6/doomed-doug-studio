Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Checked 2026-11-05.

How each source was checked:
- **Fetched** means I read the passage on the page with WebFetch (or decoded the PDF text).
- **Search** means I confirmed the claim only from search-result text quoting the page (page blocked, behind a cookie wall, or a PDF I could not open).
- **Arithmetic** means a conversion or comparison I worked out from sourced numbers. The working is shown.

## FLAGS for the screener
1. **Title claim "First" (brief section 7, Honesty).** Supported by: South Carolina Aquarium, "Survival in the wild is even more uncommon than birth, with most not surviving more than about 24 hours" (Fetched); Wild Florida co-founder Sam Haught, "In the wild, albinos would almost never survive" (phys.org, Fetched); BTO, adult albino birds in the wild "very unlikely, since bad eyesight ... means that survival prospects are poor" (Fetched); Missouri Department of Conservation, albino deer "often have poor eyesight and other disabilities that make it difficult to survive in the wild" (Fox Weather quoting MDC, Fetched). The 24-hour figure is an aquarium's estimate, not a measured study; the script attributes it ("According to the South Carolina Aquarium"). I think "First" holds; the titler does not need to fall back to "Die Young".
2. **Spoken band "Days." instead of the brief's "Minutes."** No source gives minutes for any albino animal. See script header, deviation 1.
3. **[D2: RESOLVED]** White lion hunting study. Turner, Vasicek & Somers 2015 (Open Science Repository Biology, doi:10.7392/openaccess.45011830), found by the screener and now cited in item 3. The authors work with the Global White Lion Protection Trust, and the lions were in fenced reserves, so the script says only "a fenced reserve" and "hunted about as well".
4. **Migaloo albino status.** The brief says "not settled". OMIA (quoting Polanowski et al. 2012) and Wikipedia say he is homozygous for a tyrosinase deletion, "strong evidence that Migaloo is a true albino". I could not open the paper (PubMed cookie wall), so this is Search/secondary. The script says "points to a true albino".
5. **[D2: CUT]** Warbler with one white wing (Barrowclough & Sibley 1980, The Auk). The screener's excerpts put the white feathers on both wings; the sentence and its stage direction are gone.
6. **Orcas target calves.** Search only (NOAA Institutional Repository listing of a 2016 review of 115 interactions: mammal-eating killer whales "targeted only calves"; plus Pitman et al. 2015, NOAA SWFSC PDF, on calf kills off Western Australia).

## Brief checks (corrections and cuts)
- **Snowflake:** age 38-40 (NBC) → "about forty". Cause "skin cancer" (squamous-cell carcinoma), not "the sun". The script never says the sun killed him; melanin's sun-shield role is stated as general biology.
- **Prado-Martinez:** uncle/niece is "the most probable scenario" (aunt/nephew also fits). Script: "most likely".
- **Migaloo:** "still reported in the 2020s" → last reported sighting 2020. Albino status is stronger than the brief said (FLAG 4).
- **White lion:** gene is TYR (Cho et al. 2013, Nature Communications), as the brief guessed. The hunting study is Turner, Vasicek & Somers 2015 (Open Science Repository Biology), repeated in Turner's 2023 Leiden thesis; not African Journal of Ecology.
- **Jeffery 1997:** Trends in Neurosciences, not Survey of Ophthalmology. Phrased for albino mammals only; no human eye facts anywhere.
- **Seneca:** fence 24 miles (39 km), built 1941; herd numbers vary (about 200 white per Mental Floss; about 300 per Wikipedia; about 80 in a 2016 aerial survey per OutdoorHub, Search only). The script uses "roughly two hundred" for the growth after the 1950s, not a current count.
- **Olney:** no source for "a fine for taking one out of town" or hawk predation; cut. Cats and the 2002 cat ordinance used instead.
- **Bird:** Bonser 1995 is in The Condor. The survival line is BTO's, not a direct van Grouw quote (van Grouw's papers could not be opened). No albino-bird case with a known fate was found, so none is used.
- **[D2] Band "Day one."** (was "Days."): the turtle's two days are how long it stayed in the nest ("started its life two days behind"), not a lifespan; its TIME LEFT sign reads "?". The alligator's "about 24 hours" is the only survival time in the band.
- **Turtle:** the beach-run distance and time could not be sourced, so the item uses no run-length analogy (the analogy is the egg-carton arithmetic).
- **Alligator:** "minutes" is unsourced; the sourced figure is about 24 hours (estimate). Claude died December 2025, not December 2024. Liver cancer is not mentioned.
- **Optional ninth item (white owl):** not used. Draft 2 is 1,999 words, inside 1,950-2,350.
- **People with albinism:** no mention anywhere. "Albino" is used only as an adjective or noun for animals. Banned words (freak, mutant, abnormal, defective, cursed) do not appear.
