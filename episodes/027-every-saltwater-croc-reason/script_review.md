### FAIL 1: The Lunge contradicts the source it cites
- Script lines:
  - "On land, a big crocodile is not much of an athlete."
  - "So the slow animal on the bank and the animal under Doug's branch are not the same problem. On land, it plods."
- The NT Government pages the writer cites for the tail go straight on to say: "These strong muscles also mean that for short bursts of time crocodiles can move faster than humans can on land."
- Hutchinson 2019 only supports a *relative* finding: larger crocodylians use "relatively less extreme gait kinematics". It does not say they are slow or that they plod.
- facts.md marks "On land, it plods" as "Inference", and a reliable source contradicts it.
- It is also the one line in the script a viewer could take away as false comfort about real crocodiles. That makes it a quiet safety-misinformation risk, on top of being a fact error.
