## Brief checks (flagged items and dropped facts)
- **Jump (flagged): FAILS, replaced.** See item 7. "The Jump" became "The Lunge", using only the NT Government's tail/lunge statement and Hutchinson 2019. The creative director needs to approve the rename.
- **Ramree (flagged): PASSES the cut rule.** It has two credible, attributed doubters:
  1. Platt et al. 2001, a peer-reviewed field interview study, read in full.
  2. The Sam Willis / National Geographic Channel records research, after which Guinness itself took the record out of circulation (Guinness news, Dec 2016).
  The war lines carry no jokes, no Doug, no names and no description of deaths beyond their broad causes. Wright's "spinning crocodiles" passage is deliberately not used anywhere.
- **Campbell, Dwyer, Irwin, Franklin 2010:** the "thermal window" paper (*Proc R Soc B* 277: 3837) is on freshwater crocodiles, and the Kennedy River paper (*J Anim Ecol*) is the tide study that 008 used. Neither is used here. The dive figures come from the Queensland Museum instead.
- **Cog-tooth valve / foramen of Panizza: dropped.** The shunt is real (Axelsson and Franklin 2001, *J Comp Physiol B*: https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s003600100183). But Eme et al. 2009, *J Exp Biol*, found that removing it did not change dive times in alligators (https://digitalcommons.calpoly.edu/bio_fac/159). "Lets it stay under longer" would be a contested claim, so the heart-rate figure is used instead.

  - Spennemann 2020, *Pacific Science* 74(3), reviews Pacific vagrants (https://www.bioone.org/journals/pacific-science/volume-74/issue-3/74.3.1/Cruising-the-Currents--Observations-of-Extra-Limital-Saltwater-Crocodiles/10.2984/74.3.1.full).
