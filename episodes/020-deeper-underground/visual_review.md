# Visual review: 020-deeper-underground

## Round 1: keyframes (pre-render)

Checked all 26 contact sheets (s001 to s308) and opened the questionable keyframes at full size. s001 is the thumbnail, which isn't made yet, so it was skipped.

**What works:** Doug is on-model everywhere (red cap, white head) and easy to read on every background, including the dark, red and orange ones. The deaths are cartoon only: X eyes, ghost Doug, cap left behind, pod crush. There is no blood or gore. The death counter goes up correctly from 161 to 170 (9 deaths). The depth HUD and chapter labels stay consistent. The suitcase sticker payoff (s307/s308) lands. The pictures match the narration in nearly every shot. The tone is not kids-show.

**What fails:** several text or drawing overlaps hide words or the key subject, and there is one long run of near-identical shots without Doug.

VERDICT: FAIL
