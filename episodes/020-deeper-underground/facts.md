# Facts: 020 Why the Ground Gets Deadlier the Deeper Doug Goes (working)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed below with its source. These are fiction or framing, not claims: Doug, his deaths, the
drill pod, the suitcase and stickers, the sandwich, rug and houseplant, snorkel, compass, flag, the cap on a string, the narrator's
asides ("which counts as the cool part of the day", "neither does the planet, sometimes", "her review was short"), and the two series
callbacks (016: "He has been to the deepest point on land"; 004: "Doug has been this hot before, in a cave full of giant crystals").
Arithmetic comparisons are marked "comparison", with the figures and the maths.

Checked 2026-10-16 (draft 1); revised for draft 2 the same day, applying script_review.md fixes 1-5 and the facts.md hygiene items. "Verified" means I read the claim on the page with WebFetch. "Search snippet" means I confirmed it from
search-result text quoting the page, because the page blocked WebFetch or I didn't open it. The screener should re-open those.

**Gore check:** deaths are bad air, getting lost, flood, heat, pressure and molten metal, all cut to X eyes, steam lines, the cap floating
or a blob. No injury detail. The Mobley rescue and the Veryovkina flood are sober cards with no Doug and no jokes, and both state that
everyone survived.
**Safety check:** the ancient water gets "For the record, it is not drinking water." The hot-tub line states a design limit, not advice.
**Accuracy hedges:** Movile's isolation is "scientists estimate", given as a range of two and a half to five and a half million years; the transition-zone water "might" equal the oceans and "nobody knows yet
whether that one diamond speaks for the whole planet"; the core temperature is "give or take five hundred"; the inner-core rotation is
presented as two papers plus "other scientists still disagree"; the Kola "screams" are called a hoax in one line and not described.

**Changed from the brief after checking:**
- Veryovkina is no longer stated as *the* deepest cave. Wikipedia gives 2,209 m (2024 re-survey) and lists it second after Krubera;
  Discover Wildlife says official records still give Veryovkina the title but a 2024 estimate puts Krubera at 2,224 m. The script says
  the two "keep trading the title ... a few meters at a time".
- Devil worm water temperature (37-48 C): not found in any source I could open. Cut.
- Mponeng "about an hour by cage": no solid source. Replaced with Guinness's lift figure.
- Sherwood Lollar's "salty and bitter" wording: only on weak sites. The script uses the Grist quote "It tastes terrible."
- Movile "about 20 m": sources give an 18 m shaft and 18 m of cover. The script says eighteen meters.
- Movile "half the oxygen": the sourced range is 7-10% (Wikipedia, citing Kumaresan 2014) to 7-16% (GESS Lab) against 21%. The script
  says "in places only a third of the normal oxygen".
