### Fact FAIL items
- **F7 (line 199, conflated).** *"In two thousand five, scientists described a badger-sized mammal from these beds called Repenomamus. In its stomach were the bones of a young Psittacosaurus..."*
  - *Repenomamus* (*R. robustus*) was named in 2000 and is opossum- or cat-sized. It is the species with the stomach contents.
  - The badger-sized species, *R. giganticus*, was named in 2005 in the same paper, but it did not hold the stomach contents.
  - As written, the line attributes the wrong size and the wrong naming date to the animal that ate the dinosaur.
- **F8 (line 281, overstated).** *"For the big one, the main suspect is another marine reptile called Nothosaurus."*
  - The Stuttgart release says Nothosaurus giganteus is "**one of** the main suspects".
  - The paper also names Cymbospondylus and Helveticosaurus as candidates, because their tooth spacing matches the 14.5 mm gap.
- **F9 (line 397, unsourced "only").** *"The driller only went after Cloudina"*
  - The sources show only that co-occurring Sinotubulites shells were left alone, and the driller is unidentified, so exclusivity cannot be known.
  - This is the same pattern as round-1 F3.
- **F10 (line 341, false and self-contradicting).** *"Four hundred and twenty-five million years later, they're still holding on."*
  - The specimen was ground away during study. Line 361 itself says *"The original fossil no longer exists."*
  - The line also repeats the line-323 construction ("Two hundred and seventy million years later...") two items apart.
