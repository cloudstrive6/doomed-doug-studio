# Facts: 015 The Deeper Doug Digs, the Scarier the Fossils Get (working)

Every factual claim in script.md is listed here with its source. Fiction and framing are not claims: Doug, his deaths, the
shovel, the shaft, the time machine, picnic, ash-man, costumes, breath mint, referee whistle, fishing rod, snorkel, magnifying
glass, speech bubbles, the stone cap (003 lore), the DEPTH meter values, and the narrator's asides ("nobody wanted him",
"the volcano called the match", "everyone involved got caught", "far too big to qualify" (no number spoken; Doug about 1.75 m vs a host a few millimetres long).
Everyday comparisons are marked "comparison" with the source of the everyday object's size and the arithmetic.

Checked 2026-10-12 (draft 1); revised for draft 2 after script_review.md (F1 to F6, fix 13). "Verified" = I read the claim on the page (WebFetch). "Search snippet" = confirmed from
search-result text quoting the page, because the page blocked WebFetch or was not opened; the screener should re-open these.

**Gore check:** no injury descriptions. Not used: how the wasps leave the pupa, the details of the Tanystropheus bite beyond
"tooth marks where the neck stops", the "bloody decapitation" framing in press coverage, Sue's lesions in detail.
**Medical check:** no human-health detail for Trichomonas, tapeworms or tongue worms. The only modern line for tongue worms is
where they live (airways of land animals, mostly reptiles). The NHM/Imperial note that some infect humans is deliberately not used.
**Kid-appeal check:** no baby animals drawn. The Sanajeh hatchling is one flat spoken mention, never drawn. No young rhino
(the Ashfall discovery skull is not mentioned). "Fossil dung" is said exactly twice, plus "coprolite" once.
**Overstated claims avoided:** Sue's cause of death is stated as unknown, and the 2009 infection idea is presented together
with the 2022 rebuttal. "Oldest known evidence of one animal preying on another" is attributed to the researchers (older protist predation exists, see The First Hole). The mammal-hunted-dinosaur claim
is limited to "at least some of the time" plus a named dissent. "Second three-level food chain" is attributed via National
Geographic's report of the study. Tanystropheus neck use and posture are not discussed.
