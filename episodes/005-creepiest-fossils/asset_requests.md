# Asset requests: 005 Creepiest Fossils

From the director, for the illustrator (the art director approves). House style follows `assets/library/anglerfish.json`
and `channel/art_bible.md`:
- Creatures, places and props are drawn in the detailed tier. Use outlines of 4-6, flat fills plus `spray` shading, a darker back and a
  lighter belly, and white eyes with black pupils. Everything faces **right** unless stated otherwise. Local coordinates are centred on the anchor.
- **Anchors and sizes matter.** I laid out all 211 shots with placeholder boxes at exactly the sizes and anchors below,
  and I checked the contact sheets. `bc` = anchor at the bottom-centre (the lowest point sits on y=0, so the drawing spans y -h..0).
  `c` = anchor at the centre. Sizes are local units at scale 1, given as width x height.
- **Tone:** a deadpan documentary for adults. **Baby animals (cub, mammoth, foal, wolf pup) must not be cute.** No big
  shiny eyes, no pastel nursery colours, no smiles. The **mummies** are evidence: dry, dark, shrunken, curled cartoon shapes
  with fur texture. No exposed tissue, no wounds, no blood, no gore. Think museum specimen, not a corpse.
- SIL = also used with `"silhouette": true`, so the outer outline must be clean and recognisable as a silhouette.
- Priority: A = hero or recurring (6+ shots), B = supporting, C = one-off.

There are no real photo insets in this episode (no licensed photos are in `assets/photos/`), so the drawn specimens carry
the "evidence" role. If the art director later sources CC/PD photos for the cub, Lyuba, the foal, Zhùr and the Messel
pit, I will add 4 px framed `image` insets.
