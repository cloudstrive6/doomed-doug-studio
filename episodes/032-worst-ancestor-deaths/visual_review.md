### Required fixes

**Policy / kid-appeal (must fix)**
1. **s082, s092 (director + illustrator):** The Taung child's skull is a 3-year-old's. Its eye sockets are filled with big red arrowheads, and at feed size that reads as bloody or stabbed eyes on a child's skull. Stop the arrows at the lower socket rim, and use a thin red ring or "x" marker *on the socket floor* instead of a filled arrowhead inside the socket. On s092, do the same on the Taung skull only (the monkey skull can keep it). Leave s087/s097 ("ABOUT 3 YEARS OLD" / "ABOUT 12 KG") as they are; they read as fossil facts and do not show a harmed child.
2. **s108, s109, s112–s118 (illustrator, SK 54 asset):** The skull cap seen from behind is a round tan dome with two small black dots side by side, centred low. It reads as a cute face (two eyes on a blob head), and this is a juvenile's skull. Redraw it as a recognisable *back of a skull*: show the occipital curve plus a brow/zygomatic hint or a partial outline of the rest of the skull dashed in, move the holes up and apart to match the 33 mm spacing against the size, and add a small "BACK OF SKULL" view label or a side silhouette. It must not read as eyes.
3. **s202–s203 (illustrator + director):** The hyena cub is drawn as a brown sitting teddy bear, and with the lowercase "hi friend?" bubble it pushes toward nursery tone. Redraw the cub as a dark spotted hyena pup (sloped back, spots, dark muzzle, round hyena ears) so it is plainly a hyena. Change the bubble to deadpan adult-coded text (e.g. "Hello.") or drop it and let the narration carry the beat.
