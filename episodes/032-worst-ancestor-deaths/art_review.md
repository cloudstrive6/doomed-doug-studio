## Illustrator caveats: rulings
1. **`leopard_lying` overhang: ACCEPTED, no change.** In s106 and s120 the head rests against the trunk, the belly sits on the
   branch, and the hind leg and tail drape off the tip. It reads as a leopard draped on a branch, which is how leopards lie. The
   red-glow silhouette in s106 keeps its legs and tail clear.
2. **`sk54_skull_cap` face-read: FIXED (by me).** The two holes, the regular sawtooth bottom edge and the inverted-V suture read
   as a Pac-Man ghost face, worst in s108 and s117 with the jaw hovering above. That is unacceptable on a child's skull. Changes:
   - bottom edge redrawn as an irregular, asymmetric break (higher on the left), with a matching edge band `#d8c494`;
   - lambdoid suture toned down to `#b8a272`, and its right branch now stops short, so it no longer reads as brows;
   - the side "cheek" sprays removed;
   - natural brown fracture lines moved onto the new edge.

   Hole centres, radius and extents are unchanged, and the s108 arrows still land on the holes.
3. **`hyena_cub`: FIXED (by me).** Two problems: the hedgehog spikes on the back, and the boxy post front legs. Together they made
   it read as a teddy or bear cub. Changes:
   - the five back spikes removed;
   - the front legs tapered and smoothed;
   - the muzzle lengthened by 10, with nose and mouth moved along.

   It now reads as a wary dark hyena cub on `#3a2e28`, and is not kawaii. Extents are unchanged.
4. **`taung_skull` teeth: FIXED (by me).** The six bright-white bead teeth made a Halloween grin. They are now flat and
   bone-toned (`#e6d4a6`, outline `#8a6a3a`, 12x14), on a straight row at y +76. The skull now reads as clinical. Sockets and
   holes are unchanged.

## Other fixes I made
5. **`plaster_skull_cast`:** the dark dot at the back read as an eye, and the dark wedge under the front read as a mouth, so the
   cast looked like a whale or slug. I removed the dot. The wedge is now a light-grey notch under the brow, and the brow ridge
   line is heavier (4, `#8a877e`). Plinth and anchor are unchanged.
6. **`antelope_skull`:** the white triangular teeth read as a predator's teeth. They are now blunt square molars `#efe2c0`.
