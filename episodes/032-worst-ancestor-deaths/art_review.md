3. **`hyena_cub`: FIXED (by me).** Two problems: the hedgehog spikes on the back, and the boxy post front legs. Together they made
   it read as a teddy or bear cub. Changes:
   - the five back spikes removed;

   It now reads as a wary dark hyena cub on `#3a2e28`, and is not kawaii. Extents are unchanged.
4. **`taung_skull` teeth: FIXED (by me).** The six bright-white bead teeth made a Halloween grin. They are now flat and
   bone-toned (`#e6d4a6`, outline `#8a6a3a`, 12x14), on a straight row at y +76. The skull now reads as clinical. Sockets and
