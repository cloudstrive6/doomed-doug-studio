## 2. What worked / didn't
- **Worked: YouTube started testing every new long upload in Suggested and Browse from 10-18** [F]. [I] The cause is probably account maturity plus
  topics that sit next to OctoLab/BTB videos (single famous animals, prehistoric). Impressions went up 19x and CTR rose to 3.7%, so the packaging works on cold audiences.
- **Didn't: the first minute on cold traffic.** AVD fell from 230 s to 93 s, and 018/019/021 keep only 6–9% of viewers at the 50% mark [F].
  [I] The cause is the hook. 020 opens on its weirdest fact ("a cave that had no entrance at all") and keeps 73% at 30 s and 55% at 10%. 018 opens on "Harmless."
  and its mildest item (the boom), and 021 on "Snacks." and a salmon; both are around 22% at 10%. Ascending-severity lists put the dull item first, and Browse viewers leave.
- **Watch-hour math [I]:** watch hours = impressions × CTR × AVD. Impressions now come from YouTube. Getting AVD from 93 s back to the 230 s level of 10-11 would
  take long watch hours from about 4.5 to about 11 per day at today's views, and better retention is also what makes Browse keep testing us.
